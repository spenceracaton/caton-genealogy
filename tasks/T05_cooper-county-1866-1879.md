# T05 — Cooper County, Missouri, 1866–1879

**Priority: 5.**

The direct line lived in Cooper County for thirteen years: William apprenticed at
Boonville, married there in 1869, and left for Kansas in October 1879. Aaron was
alive at the start of that period. **Look for him beside his son.**

**Targets:**
- 1870 federal census, Cooper County — Aaron, tailor, ~50
- Boonville city and county records 1866–1879: directories, tax, deed, probate, church, cemetery
- Local newspapers for the period

**Handle with care.** Cooper County also holds George W. Caton the Boonville
tailor, the William/Aaron F. Caton household, and Joseph Caton in later life.
Keeping those identities separate is still mandatory — see the do-not-merge list
in `BRIEF.md` §6 — but the county is no longer treated as an unrelated cluster.
Proximity is now a reason to look, not a reason to discard.

Report any record placing two of these men in the same transaction, household,
church, or witness list. That is the shape a kinship proof would take.

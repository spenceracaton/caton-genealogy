## Existing original deed retained in the repository

The retained image of Cooper Deed Book Q, p.260 was inspected again. It is a
recorded deed from James Deckard and Christina his wife. The material wording is:

> `for and in consideration of the sum of one hundred dollars in hand paid by
> Thomas Cayton for the use and benefit of Elizabeth his wife & the heirs of her
> body`

It conveys to Elizabeth and the heirs of her body forty acres described as the
NE1/4 of the NE1/4 of section 35, township 46, range 19. James and Christina
Deckard signed by mark. Both acknowledged the deed on 10 October 1850 before
J. A. Johnston, J.P.; Christina was examined apart from her husband and released
her dower. The instrument was filed and recorded 7 February 1853 in Deed Book Q,
p.260. The margin states delivery to `Thos Caton` on 4 March 1853.

Source image: FamilySearch permanent image
<https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLK-6SM7-H>, formerly
identified as image 443 of 653; local files
`evidence/1850_Cooper_DeedQ_p260_ElizabethCaton.jpg` and
`evidence/1850_Cooper_DeedQ_p260_ElizabethCaton_crop.jpg`.

IDENTITY LIMIT: This deed names Elizabeth as the wife of one Thomas and protects
her heirs, but it gives no age, occupation, birthplace, suffix, named child,
Maddex associate, signature of Thomas, or prior residence. It cannot be assigned
to the older tailor. Its parcel chain is still the most specific adjacent-chain
test for the younger Thomas–Elizabeth household.

## New direct evidence: Aaron Caton in 1850

Aaron Caton's 1850 household has now been located in the original census image.

Source: 1850 U.S. census, Delaware County, Ohio, Delaware Town, page no. 33, stamped 179; NARA M432 roll 675; Internet Archive item `populationschedu0675unix`, image n172.

- Enumeration date: 22 August 1850

- Page: No. 33; stamped 179

Household, lines 38-42; dwelling/family appear to be 1162/1163:

| Line | Name | Age | Sex | Occupation | Birthplace | Notes |

| 38 | Aaron Caton | 30 | M | Tailor | Virginia | Direct-line Aaron; confirms VA-born tailor in 1850 |
| 39 | Sarah Caton | 26 | F | - | Ohio | Wife of Aaron |
| 40 | Julia Caton | 1 | F | - | Appears to read Missouri | Needs independent recheck before using as proof of an early Missouri trip |
| 41 | Mary E. Caton | 5 | F | - | Ohio | New direct child/search key |
| 42 | Wm B. Caton | 2 | M | - | Ohio | Direct-line William Bramwell Caton |

Interpretation:
- This is primary evidence that Aaron was in Delaware County, Ohio in August 1850.
- It independently confirms the key identifiers: Aaron, wife Sarah, son William B., Virginia birthplace, and tailoring occupation.
- It adds Mary E. Caton and Julia Caton as evidence-backed household members and future search keys.
- The repeated compiled claim of a daughter Nancy Roseman Caton born 10 November 1849 is not supported by this household as written. Nancy may be a false/conflated child, may have died before the census, may have been in another household, or may reflect a name error in later compiled material. Do not treat Nancy as confirmed from this census.
- Julia's birthplace appears to be written as Missouri, while Mary E. and William B. are written as Ohio. Because that would materially change the migration timeline, verify Julia's birthplace against another image/index before treating it as proven.

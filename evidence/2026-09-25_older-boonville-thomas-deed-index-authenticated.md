QUESTION TESTED: Can Cooper County deeds identify the 77-year-old
Virginia-born tailor Thomas Caton in John P. Maddex's 1850 Boonville household
through a wife, Maddex relationship, parcel, signature, prior residence, heir,
or other direct identity fact?

- the 77-year-old Virginia-born tailor in John P. Maddex's Boonville household;

## Book Q, p.260 parcel control

The retained Book Q original was reread with the now-verified paired index
entries. James Deckard and Christina his wife acknowledged a conveyance made
for $100 paid by Thomas Cayton/Caton for the use and benefit of Elizabeth his
wife and the heirs of her body. The grantee is Elizabeth. The parcel is the
NE1/4 of the NE1/4 of section 35, township 46, range 19, forty acres. The deed
was acknowledged 10 October 1850, filed and recorded 7 February 1853, and the
margin states delivery to `Thos Caton` on 4 March 1853.

This deed supplies the wife relationship but no age, occupation, birthplace,
Maddex associate, named child, prior residence, or Thomas signature. It remains
unassigned between the older tailor and other same-name Thomas identities.

## Book S, pp.59–60 Thomas-to-Trigg deed

The original begins on manuscript p.59 and ends on p.60. It is dated 14 March
1855 and names **Thomas Caton** as party of the first part and **William H.
Trigg** as party of the second part. For $30, Thomas conveyed:

> the East half of Lot No 3 of North East quarter of section one Township
> Forty five Range nineteen containing Forty 7/100 acres

The instrument contains a handwritten `Thomas Caton` signature rather than a
mark. Clerk Benj. Tompkins recorded that Thomas was personally known to him as
the subscriber, that Thomas acknowledged the deed on 14 March 1855, and that
the instrument was recorded in Deed Book S, pp.59–60.

The S parcel is not the Q parcel: section 1, township 45, range 19 is distinct

age, occupation, birthplace, prior residence, or Maddex associate. The
signature cannot identify the older tailor without a comparison signature from
an independently identified record.

## Maddox/Maddex control and variant-search limits

The original M-continuation spread at manuscript pp.504–505, FamilySearch image
273, was inspected. It includes several J. P. Maddox entries, including J. P.
Maddox and W. Poston as guardians in a bond to E. Hart at p.339 and J. P.
Maddox and wife to E. E. Hammond at p.468. The spread does not spell out
Parthema and does not name a Caton co-party. Initials alone do not prove that
these entries belong to the 1850 householder John P. Maddex.

## Coverage boundary and result

The authenticated pass verifies Q:260 in both C and D sections and S:59 in the
C section. It does **not** establish that every C/K/M leaf was read line by
line, does not supply the inverted Trigg entry, and does not complete the
section-35 or section-1 successor chain through Books T–Z. Those are unexamined
coverage, not negative evidence.

No original reviewed here links either deed Thomas to the older Boonville
tailor, the younger District 23 farmer, John P. Maddex, Aaron Caton, or Joseph
Caton. No parent or child is named. The 1855 deed is a durable same-name record
and signature specimen only.

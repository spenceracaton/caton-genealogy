# A2: Fauquier marriage search remains sign-in blocked

## Research question

Test A2 in `PARENTAGE-TESTS.md` asks whether Thomas Caton Senior had a second marriage in Fauquier or a neighboring county during 1810–1819. A second marriage could identify a widow, guardian, or family context relevant to Aaron Caton's parentage.

## Source and retrieval

- Virginia Library of Virginia, Fauquier County microfilm catalog: <https://www.lva.virginia.gov/collections/ccmf/VA/VA083>
- The catalog identifies the relevant source as `Marriage Bonds and Returns, Vol. 3, 1805–1820`, on reel 66, with an index to volumes 1–6 on reel 65.
- FamilySearch Virginia Marriage Records Index, 1785–1940: <https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1708698>
- A direct FamilySearch record query for `Thomas Caton`, Virginia, marriage-like date 1810–1819 redirected to the FamilySearch identity sign-in page before returning any result, image, or film reference.
- A live direct-image check on 15 September 2026 used the related FamilySearch Fauquier marriage-volume surface (`https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L9XF-6BPB`, volume 2) as an access test. It redirected to FamilySearch identity sign-in before exposing an image. This was not treated as the target volume 3 and no credentials were entered.
- The public Prince William County compilation, `Marriage Records of Residents of Prince William County, Virginia, 1731–1930`, was checked through its searchable public mirror. Its alphabetical C index runs from `CATOE` to `CAUTHORN` without a `CATON` entry. The county library describes this compilation as drawn from sources other than the official marriage registers, so it is a control for derivative records only.
- Public mirror used for the page-level check: <https://www.yumpu.com/en/document/view/46327260/marriages-file-eservices-of-prince-william-county-virginia/4>

## Finding boundary

No marriage result or image was retrieved. The sign-in redirect is an access failure, not a negative search result. The LVA catalog metadata establishes the exact next source target, but it does not establish whether Thomas Caton appears in it.

The live image-surface check confirms that the present FamilySearch route remains blocked at sign-in. Because the tested ARK is a related Fauquier volume rather than volume 3, it confirms the access boundary only; it supplies no handwriting or index evidence about Thomas Caton.

The public Prince William transcriptions, deed/index files, and the non-official marriage compilation checked in this session produced no usable Thomas/Aaron Caton marriage entry. Those derivative and partial indexes are not treated as a negative for the surviving official register and do not alter A2.

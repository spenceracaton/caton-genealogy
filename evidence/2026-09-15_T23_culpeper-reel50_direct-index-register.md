Use the direct Reel 50 images rather than relying only on Full-Text Search. The

## Source and direct observations

- The Library of Virginia catalog describes Reel 50 as containing the index to the
  Culpeper marriage register and the marriage register itself. It also states that
  **Marriage Register No. 1 was not filmed**. [LVA Reel 50 guide](https://www.lva.virginia.gov/collections/ccmf/VA/VA067)
- FamilySearch's direct surname-index image 32 reads: `Caton, Mary E., 37.`
  The same page has the cross-reference `Caton see also Kayton.` No Caton entry
  naming Aaron, Thomas, Moses, a parent, or a consent appears on that page.
  [FamilySearch index image 32](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G9XF-KSLC?view=fullText&lang=en&groupId=M9NK-KVT&cat=true)
- The direct register image 208, printed page 37, contains the row dated **16
  March 1847** for **John C. Foushee** and **Mary E. Caton**. The FamilySearch
  name view gives the same household pair. This is a real Culpeper marriage
  record, but it is a collateral record and does not name Mary's parents.
  [FamilySearch register image 208](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-99XF-K342?view=fullText&lang=en&groupId=M9NK-KVY&cat=true)
- The focused direct-register image 194 (printed page markers 15–16) contains
  1815–1819 entries including 3 January 1816 William Brown, 21 November 1816
  Thomas Bras, 30 August 1817 James Baldrick, 11 September 1817 Willis Browning,
  18 of 1817 George Beale, and 1819 entries for Osborn Beatty, Jacob Baker, and
  Archibald Barker. No Caton relationship appears on this reviewed page.
  This page is a bounded page-level check, not a complete negative for every
  marriage in 1816–1819.
  [FamilySearch focused register image](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L9XF-KSBW?view=fullText&lang=en&groupId=M9NK-KVY&cat=true)

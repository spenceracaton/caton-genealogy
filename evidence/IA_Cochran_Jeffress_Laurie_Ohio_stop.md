# Cochran/Jeffress Family History — Laurie Ohio Stop and Fauquier Origin

**Source:** *Histories of John Richard and Polly Saunders Jeffress and Their Descendants*
by Mary E. Cochran, 1960.
**IA identifier:** `historiesofjohnr00coch`
**Accessed:** 14–15 Sep 2026 via `be-api.us.archive.org/fts/v1/search`

## Key extracts (IA full-text snippets)

1. **Catherine Laurie** born "probably in Fauquier County" Virginia, Jan 8, 1771.
   Married Joseph Laurie.

2. **Daniel Laurie** (b. c.1806 VA) appears in 1850 Cooper County, Missouri census,
   age 44 — consistent with a Virginia birth.

3. **Daniel Laurie's son Joseph** was born in Ohio c.1835. His daughter was born in
   Missouri c.1838 (from census data cross-referenced with C098, C126).

## Significance

The Laurie family of Cooper County originated in Fauquier County, Virginia. Daniel
Laurie's son Joseph's birth in Ohio c.1835 confirms a stop in Ohio between Virginia and
Missouri — exactly matching the Fauquier Caton cluster's known migration route
(Fauquier VA → Ohio c.1835 → Cooper Co. MO c.1838). The specific Ohio county where
Joseph Laurie was born is NOT named in the Cochran book. If it was Delaware County, it
would explain how Aaron Caton met Sarah Ann Gardner (PARENTAGE-TESTS §B14).

Ann F. (Lawrey/Laurie/Laury) Caton, wife of William Caton of Cooper County, was from
this same Fauquier Laurie family. Catherine Laurie's Fauquier origin strengthens the
case that the William–Ann–Laurie cluster and Aaron all descend from the Fauquier
Caton household of Thomas Sr.

## Material transcription

### Sue Caton Dumm form, 791-10-4d

The heading identifies `Sue (Mrs. James M.) Dumm` as informant. On the informant side, the line for her brothers and sisters reads:

> They Remained in Missouri. 1 Bro. Otis O. Caton is in Salisbury Missouri and Sister Mrs. Sarah Schanz in San Diego, Calif.

This names Sue's siblings, not Joseph's. On the veteran line she writes:

> Father Joseph Caton - in Mexican War and Home Guard in Civil War -

The form does not name Joseph's parents, siblings, birthplace, county, or relationship to Aaron. The additional narrative discusses James Monroe Dumm's Missouri-to-Colorado migration, marriage to Sue, work, property, and death. It adds no older Caton generation or Virginia place.

### Clint O. Dumm form, BHS 328-1-85

The first page identifies `CLINT OTIS DUMM` as informant and names his parents as `JAMES MONROE P. DUMM` and `SUE CATON DUMM`. Beside the parents' move from Missouri, a marginal note distinguishes `He - Carroll Co` and `She - Saline Co`; this concerns where Clint's parents came from when moving to Colorado and is not a statement about Joseph's Virginia birthplace. Clint's sibling line names only Wylie Edgar Dumm. Joseph, Aaron, and an older Caton generation do not appear.

The Clint form is not a second account of Joseph's ancestry. It overlaps with Sue's form on the Dumm migration and immediate household, but it contains no statement capable of testing Joseph's parents, siblings, Virginia county, or relationship to Aaron.

### Printed James Monroe Dumm biography, BHS 823-1-67

The supplied pages are numbered 658-659 and headed `PORTRAIT AND BIOGRAPHICAL RECORD`. Page 659 identifies Sue as a Brunswick native and daughter of Joseph and Harriet (Dumm) Caton. The material Caton passage states, in part:

> The Catons were an old Virginia family ... Joseph Caton was born in Virginia

It then places Joseph in the tailor's trade in Brunswick and says he was living in Boonville when the sketch was written. It names the five children who reached maturity: Eugene DeCourcey, Thomas Edgar, Otis O., Mrs. Sallie Shantz, and Sue by context as James Dumm's wife. It does not name Joseph's parents or siblings, identify a Virginia county, identify any Revolutionary-era Caton, or mention Aaron.

The broad Virginia statement is consistent with Joseph's other records but does not adjudicate Fauquier versus Fairfax, connect Joseph to Aaron, or establish a shared parent.

## Provenance and restrictions

The archive reply is private correspondence and is not reproduced here. The current Carnegie research policy confirms that remote patrons may request research and copies and that staff generally provide no more than ten pages of scans by email. It does not expressly authorize public republication of supplied scans. The three PDFs therefore remain outside this public repository. This evidence note preserves the record identities, received filenames, page counts, hashes, source readings, and access boundary without depositing the images.

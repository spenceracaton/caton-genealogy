## Findings and limits

### Pension identity

The high-resolution card reading settles the unit dispute without T289. The first character is `E`, and the numeral is `2`; the line reads `E. 2 Mo. Cav.` The filing date reads `12 Apr. 76`. The prior note's `Co. C, 3rd Mo. Cav.` and `12 June 1876` readings are not defensible against the retained image.

The card is accepted as Aaron's with high confidence. It remains an index, not the application. `Invalid` and a blank certificate establish that this card does not record an issued certificate; they do not establish why the claim failed, whether testimony was filed, or whether a separate dependent application exists.

### Complete federal service record

NARA lists M405 as the complete Missouri Union CMSR series in Record Group 94 and identifies Fold3, Ancestry, and FamilySearch as digitization partners: [NARA partner table](https://www.archives.gov/digitization/digitized-by-partners). Opening page 1 reached image 200446828 and the message: `To view this image you need a Fold3 membership.` FamilySearch collection 1932408 reached its sign-in screen. No jacket image was read. The public title's age 44 matches the existing retained service abstract and reinforces that the located jacket is the correct Aaron.

NARA describes a CMSR as a jacket containing cards abstracted from muster, pay, hospital, and related records, sometimes with original personal papers. Its guidance states that these records do not normally identify parents or next of kin: [NARA CMSR guidance](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/army/compiled-military-service-records). The task's suggestion that the jacket may carry next-of-kin is therefore possible only through an unusual retained personal paper, not a normal CMSR field.

### Biographical and family evidence recovered

- a residence before Chillicothe, Missouri;
- a parent, sibling, or other relative;
- an occupation beyond the existing project evidence;
- a statement from anyone who knew Aaron before 1841.

The pension application is more promising than the CMSR for the research question because federal pension files commonly contain declarations, personal information, and supporting testimony. The identity match raises the expected value of obtaining application 233783 but does not itself strengthen Fauquier over any other Virginia origin.

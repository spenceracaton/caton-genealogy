GRADE: C for genealogical interpretation. These are index citations only; no underlying article image was retrieved. The John entries do not establish residence or kinship.

NEW FACTS:

- John Caton | SHSMO's index cites two unclaimed-letter notices at the Boonville post office | 10 Oct 1828 and 10 Apr 1829 | Boonville, Missouri | C-level lead only.
- Aaron Caton | SHSMO's index lists a death notice in the 18 Jan 1878 Boonville Weekly Advertiser | 18 Jan 1878 | Boonville, Missouri | confirms the existing C108 citation only; adds no article text.

CONFLICTS: None. The 1886 Aaron citation was not merged with the direct-line Aaron; it may refer to the collateral Aaron F. Caton.

NEGATIVE RESULTS:

- `Thos Caton`, `Thos. Caton`, `Jno Caton`, and `Jno. Caton` returned no matches in the selected SHSMO index. `Caton, Thos` returned only the 1868 Perche voter-registration item and the 1880 Thomas D. item above. This is a bounded index result, not a newspaper-wide negative.
- Prior exact searches in this index found no `Caton, Moses` entry; `Caton, Thomas` returned only Thomas D. Caton in an 1882 Columbia Statesman jury item. Neither result identifies the older Virginia Thomas.
- SHSMO's Cooper County directory places the Boonville Weekly Advertiser run covering 18 Jan 1878 on reel 1744 (4 Jan 1878–26 Dec 1879).
- SHSMO's Howard County directory places the Missouri Intelligencer and Boon's Lick Advertiser run covering both John Caton citations on reel 10951 (31 May 1827–9 Apr 1830).
- The official SHSMO Newspapers.com referral returned `Unauthorized Access` and directed the user back to the institution portal to sign in again. No image was examined and no access-control workaround was attempted.
- SHSMO offers exact-citation article requests for $4 per citation, or microfilm ILL for 1–2 reels at $15 within the United States for two weeks. No request or payment was made.
- LVA's public catalog confirms Prince William Bond Books 1815–1826 and 1815–1833 on reel 34, both with unpaged indexes, and marks the reel available for ILL. No target image was retrieved. LVA's free Virtual Reading Room allows one reel of microfilm in a one-hour Zoom session and up to ten page photographs; it requires an appointment and excludes published newspapers. It is a potential remote route for reel 34, not for the Missouri newspaper issues. No appointment was requested.
- Virginia Chronicle opened to a Cloudflare `Just a moment...` interstitial; no item-level search ran. This is an access block, not a negative result.

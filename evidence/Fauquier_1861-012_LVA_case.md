# Fauquier Chancery 1861-012 — John N. Caton and the Lawrie family

## Source

- Repository: Library of Virginia, Chancery Records Index, Fauquier County, index no. 1861-012.
- Case style in the scanned file: `Caton & wife & als. v. Bussey's Exor. & als.`
- Index: https://old.lva.virginia.gov/chancery/case_detail.asp?CFN=061-1861-012
- Local preservation: `Fauquier_1861-012_LVA_case.zip` (40 scanned pages downloaded from the Library of Virginia).
- The index dates the originating estate record to 1843; the suit papers and decrees run through 1861.

## Direct observations from the original scans

- The bill names the plaintiffs as **John N. Caton and Sarah Elizabeth, his wife**; **Richard Burke and Mary J., his wife**; and **George W. Lawrie**, an infant represented by Newton Vowles (scans 0003–0004).
- It states that Henry Bussey's will gave his daughter **Susan Lawrie** $800, payable by his son/executor William H. Bussey after Henry's death. The will itself names Susan Lawrie and Sarah Hunt as Henry's daughters (scan 0027; will dated 17 September 1842 and proved 27 March 1843).
- The commissioner reported the $800 legacy was to be divided in three equal parts: one to **John N. Caton and Sarah E., his wife**; one to **Richard Burke and Mary J., his wife**; and one to Newton Vowles as guardian of **George W. Lawrie** (scans 0020–0021).
- A guardianship record within the case calls George W. Lawrie a nonresident Virginia infant under fourteen and orphan of **Newton Lawrie**, deceased (scans 0039–0040). Together with the bill, this identifies Sarah Elizabeth Lawrie, Mary J. Lawrie, and George W. Lawrie as the three children/representatives of the Newton Lawrie–Susan Lawrie branch.

## Scope and implication

This is a primary-record proof that a John N. Caton married Sarah Elizabeth Lawrie in Fauquier County by 1860/61. It establishes a later Caton–Lawrie connection in the county. It does **not** name Aaron Caton, Joseph Caton, Thomas Caton, William Caton, or Ann Caton, and therefore does not prove a relationship to Aaron's target family. Treat it as collateral context only; do not merge this John N. Caton into Aaron's line.

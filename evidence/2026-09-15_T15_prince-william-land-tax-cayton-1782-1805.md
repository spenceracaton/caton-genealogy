# T15 — Prince William land-tax candidate: `Cayton, Thomas`

STATUS: partial

## Question

Does the early Prince William land-tax series contain a Caton/Cayton entry
that could identify the generation above the Broad Run Caton cluster?

## Source and retrieval

- [FamilySearch catalog record: Land tax lists of Prince William County
  [Virginia] 1782–1861](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:410825)
- The catalog identifies film 29960, item 1, DGS 7893689 as `Land tax lists,
  1782-1807, 1809-1816`; the FamilySearch image-group metadata labels item 1
  `Land Estate Tax Records 1782–1805`.
- The image group is explicitly described as having no index. Its citation
  identifies image 288 of 400: [image-level record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS4C-SKSY?view=explore&keywords=Cayton&searchForm=advanced&lang=en&groupId=M9NH-JCT).
- Authenticated Full-Text Search within DGS 7893689 returned:
  - `Caton`: 0 results.
  - `White`: 48 results, used as a control showing that this group has
    searchable transcript content.
  - `Cayton`: 1 result, the image cited above.

## Image-level observation

After enlarging and panning image 288, the handwritten row in the surname and
given-name columns is readable as:

> Cayton | Thomas

The row's tax columns are present, but the numeric entries were not used. The
image itself does not expose a reliable year heading at the row; the result is
therefore dated only to the catalogued image group, not to a specific tax year.

## Finding and boundary

- The early Prince William land-tax image group contains a handwritten
  `Cayton, Thomas` candidate.
- The candidate is relevant to the Broad Run origin hypothesis, but it does
  not establish that this man is the same person as the Thomas Caton in the
  1797–1799 cluster, nor that he is Aaron Caton's ancestor.
- The FamilySearch search-layer metadata displayed `Years: 1821` for this
  result, which conflicts with the catalogued 1782–1805 item description and
  falls outside the stated group. That metadata was not adopted as a date.
- `Caton` returning zero is a bounded full-text result, not negative proof:
  handwritten OCR may miss the spelling, and the catalog states that the
  image group has no index.

GRADE: A for the image-level `Cayton | Thomas` reading; C for identity,
relationship, and exact-year implications.

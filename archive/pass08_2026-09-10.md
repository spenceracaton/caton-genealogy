## Tooling failure that nearly poisoned the file
`grep` is a shell function wrapping ugrep with `-I`. The transcriptions are ISO-8859,
so it silently skips them as binary — empty output, exit 1, identical in appearance to
a real no-match. The first Roseman and Gardner searches returned false negatives this
way and were nearly recorded. **Use `command grep -a` in this folder, and always
verify with a control term known to be present.** Recorded as C036.

**The reversal was right after all.** The withdrawal reasoning was sound and its
conclusion wrong: the 1860 enumerator IS unreliable — he happened to be correct about

- A proofread transcription does NOT automatically beat an image read when the cell is
  **damaged**. The transcriber normalised a blotted "Mo" into the "Mass" they had
  written eight times on the same sheet. **Merge rule 3 needs that caveat.**
- The parallel thread of 10 Sep was right about the birthplace and wrong only about the
  age. C016 discarded the entire reading because one part of it was wrong.

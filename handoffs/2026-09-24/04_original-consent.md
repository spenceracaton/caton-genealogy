### Why this matters

The original 1830 Fauquier census has now been checked.

It shows:
- Thomas Caton Jun = male 20–30
- William Caton = male 20–30
- Thomas Caton Sen = male 60–70

Taken literally and assuming the men are correctly identified and the ages accurate, these brackets do not support Thomas Jun as William’s father. Census or consent errors, changing suffix usage, and different identities must be evaluated rather than excluded by assumption.

Possible explanations include:

A. The typescript incorrectly added “Jr.” Removing it would remove a conflict but would not, by itself, identify the signer as the older Thomas.

B. A different adult Thomas existed who did not head an 1830 census household.

C. Suffix usage changed between documents, or an age, identity, or other documentary reading is wrong. Junior/senior are not permanent person identifiers.

Do not choose among these before seeing the original.

## Norlin Library provenance lead

Carnegie's `Clint Otis Dumm papers` collection-level record says related material exists at the Archives, Norlin Library, University of Colorado Boulder. The current CU Boulder ArchivesSpace catalog resolves that reference as COU:545, `Clint O. Dumm Papers`, 1897–1906, three linear feet in one box.

The complete inventory contains five folders: cancelled checks; small-scale locomotive photographs and rough drawings; master locomotive drawings; blueprint folios and loose blueprints; and researcher-use blueprint copies. A keyword `Dumm` search across the whole CU archive returned only COU:545 and unrelated F. E. Dumm sheet music. The Norlin lead therefore does not supply a duplicate 1932 family-history record on the present finding aid.

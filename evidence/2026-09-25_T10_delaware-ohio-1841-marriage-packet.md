# T10 — Delaware County, Ohio, 1841 marriage packet

**Research date:** 25 September 2026  
**People:** Aaron Caton and Sarah Ann Gardner  
**Scope:** Phase 1 of `tasks/T10_missouri-1843.md`: retrieve and assess the
original marriage record and every online surviving associated paper. No
canonical registry, brief, task frontmatter, or prior evidence file was edited.

## Question and stopping rule

Could the 7–8 August 1841 marriage material name a parent, consentor, surety,
witness, residence, or other associate who bears on Aaron Caton's parentage or
the family's later Missouri route?

The test succeeds only if an original or image-verified record supplies such a
name or place. An index, later typescript, same-name entry, or officiant's local
residence cannot establish kinship. Failure to locate a loose packet online is
an access boundary, not proof that no packet survives.

## Prior repository state checked

- C005 already transcribes the retained original register image and identifies
  the officiant as Titus Case, M.G.
- C178 identifies Titus Case as a Christian Church elder associated with the
  Orange/Liberty Township area. That is Gardner-side local context, not a link
  to Aaron's parents.
- T10 rests on C037/C046 for the later Missouri window. This pass did not extend
  into Missouri because the marriage record exposed no new migration associate.

## 1. FamilySearch index and original register

### Index / locator checked first

FamilySearch's indexed entry, `Ohio, County Marriages, 1789–2016`, names Aaron
Caton and Sarah Gardner, marriage 8 August 1841, source detail `p 291-3`, frame

The index supplies no age, parent, residence, witness, consentor, surety, or
bond information. It was used only to locate the original.

Image-verified transcription:

> Aaron Caton & Sarah Gardner lic'd Augt. 7th 1841—  
> State of Ohio Delaware County ss  
> On the 8th day of August A.D. 1841 I solemnized the marriage of Aaron
> Caton with Sarah Gardner

> Ret'd & Recorded Oct. 15th 1841

The entry names only the couple, officiant Titus Case, and recording clerk W. D.
Heim. It gives no age, parent, residence, witness, consentor, surety, signature
of either spouse, or prior locality.

Authenticated adjacent-image inspection established the physical sequence:

- image 159: bound register pages 288–289;
- image 160: bound register pages 290–291, including the target entry;
- image 161: bound register pages 292–293.

This is a continuous bound record book. No reverse, attachment, application,
bond, consent, or loose return is interfiled with page 291. That does **not**
establish that separate loose papers never existed or do not survive elsewhere.

## 2. Repository-supplied derivative checked before OCR

FamilySearch catalog 181176 describes *The early marriage bonds of Delaware
County, Ohio, 1832–1865* as a microreproduction of a three-volume typescript
written in 1940 and held in the State Library of Ohio D.A.R. Collection. The
catalog identifies film 317362 / DGS 7900607 and links an online copy of volume
1:

- catalog: <https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/181176>
- digital volume 1: <https://www.familysearch.org/library/books/records/item/1055886-the-early-marriage-bonds-of-delaware-county-ohio-1832-1865>
- target: viewer image 105, typescript page numbered 97

The retained full page and one inspection crop are:

- `evidence/2026-09-25_T10_DAR_early-marriage-bonds_v1_image105.png`
- `evidence/2026-09-25_T10_DAR_early-marriage-bonds_v1_image105_AaronSarah_crop.png`

The typed entry appears to read:

> Gardner, Sarah m Aaron Eaton  
> Aug. 8, 1841 by Titus Case, M.G.

The book's internal search returned the entry for `Aaron` but no result for
`Caton`. The original register plainly reads `Caton`, so the apparent `Eaton`
form is a derivative transcription error, not an alternate identity. The
typescript adds no surety, witness, consentor, parent, residence, or signature.
OCR was not used to establish the wording; the image and crop were inspected.

## 3. Online coverage and access boundary

The FamilySearch original-record catalog exposes the bound marriage volumes,
including volume 1. It does not list a loose-license, bond, consent, or packet
series for this marriage. The separate D.A.R. work is a 1940 typescript, not an
image set of original loose bonds.

Delaware County Memory collection 29 is identified by current county and local
history pages as containing *Marriage Records Vol. 1, 1835–1845*. Its public
site refused the live connection during this pass. That collection is another
copy of the record-book route; no online catalog or image located in this pass
identified a loose packet for Aaron and Sarah.

**Result:** the bound original is retrieved and fully reviewed. A separate
license/bond/consent packet was not retrieved, and its survival remains unknown.
This is incomplete access, not a negative search of the county's physical
holdings.

## 5. Parentage and migration assessment

- **Titus Case:** already identified in C178 as a Christian Church elder in the
  Orange/Liberty area. His role supports a Gardner-side local setting only. An
  officiant is not evidence of kinship to Aaron and supplies no Missouri route.
- **W. D. Heim:** appears only as the recording clerk. This administrative role
  does not make him a witness or associate of the couple.
- **No surety, witness, or consentor is named** in the online original or the
  later typescript. The surviving loose-paper question must be answered by the
  physical custodian.
- **No parentage or migration inference changes.** The marriage still fixes
  Aaron in Delaware County by 7 August 1841 and the couple's marriage on 8
  August, but supplies no pre-1841 Aaron locality and no person to carry into
  Phase 2 of T10.

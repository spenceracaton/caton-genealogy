# Fauquier overseers and marriage-paper access check — 17 September 2026

## Scope

Public-source check for the primary-proof stream: Fauquier County Board of
Overseers of the Poor Minutes, 1804–1845 (Library of Virginia Reel 116 / barcode
1125621), with a secondary check of the William Caton–Ann F. Lawrey and Daniel
Laury–Ann Caton marriage-paper references.

## Findings

1. The ARVAS/LVA finding aid identifies one volume, **Fauquier County (Va.)
   Board of Overseers of the Poor Minutes, 1804–1845**, held by the Library of
   Virginia, microfilm **Fauquier County Reel 116**, collection barcode
   **1125621**. The finding aid describes minutes concerning poor relief,
   poorhouse matters, levies, burials, and **binding children as apprentices**.
   It reports **0 items available online** and supplies no name index or page
   images. This is a catalog-level access result, not a negative search of the
   volume.

2. The Library of Virginia’s Fauquier microfilm table independently lists Reel
   116 as “Overseer of the Poor Minute Book, 1804, 1845.” No digitized images or
   searchable transcription are exposed from that public page. The record
   series remains a valid lead for an apprenticeship, relief, burial, or
   guardianship-style entry involving Caton children, but no Caton, Aaron,
   Joseph, Thomas, William, or Lawrey relationship was verified from the
   minutes in this pass.

3. Fauquier Public Library’s public index to **Marriage Returns (Not Bonds),
   1785–1848** lists `Caton, Ann` at return page 36 and `Caton, Thomas` and
   `Caton, William` at return page 27; it also lists `Laury, Daniel` at page 36
   and `Lawry, Ann F.` and `Lawry, Elizabeth` at page 27. This corroborates the
   existence and index placement of the already-known 1828–35 marriage-return
   entries, but the index is not the original bond/consent and does not add a
   relationship statement.

## Negative scope and access boundary

No image-level reading was possible here for Reel 116 or the FamilySearch
marriage-paper images. Therefore this pass establishes **no documentary
negative** for Caton entries in the overseers’ minutes and does not alter the
existing conclusion that the marriage papers name Thomas Jr. as William’s
father and Thomas Sr. as Ann’s father, while naming neither Aaron nor Joseph.

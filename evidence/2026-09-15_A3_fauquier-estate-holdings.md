# A3 — Fauquier probate, 1835–1844: public holdings check

STATUS: blocked

## Question

Does a Fauquier probate record for the elder Thomas Caton name Aaron, Joseph, or
other heirs after Thomas Sr.'s documented 1835 appearance and before the family's
dispersal?

## Record and access result

No Thomas Caton probate page, will-book index entry, or estate image was retrieved
in this pass. The Library of Virginia's Fauquier County microfilm guide identifies
the relevant holdings as:

- Will Book 13, 1832–1835, reel 36
- Will Book 14, 1835–1836, reel 36
- Will Book 15, 1836–1838, reel 37
- Will Book 16, 1838–1840, reel 37
- Will Book 17, 1840–1842, reel 38
- Will Book 18, 1842–1844, reel 38

The same guide marks these microfilm copies as available for loan. The Fauquier
County Record Room states that its will books run from 1759 to the present and
accepts written requests for copies when the decedent's full name and date of death
are supplied. Neither route produced a record-level image or index entry here.

## Finding and limit

This is a holdings and access result, not a negative probate result. It does not
support or weaken the hypothesis that Thomas Sr. left an estate naming Aaron or
Joseph. A3 remains open for a library-accessible microfilm copy, a local record-room
search, or a public image route.

# A2 — Culpeper marriage register, 1781–1853: bounded 1816–1819 search

## Question

Does the accessible Culpeper marriage compilation identify Thomas Caton's
second marriage, approximately 1816–1819, or a spelling-variant entry that
could identify Aaron's mother?

## Retrieval

The authenticated FamilySearch session showed `Account: Spencer5362`. The
catalog record is [Register of marriages, Culpeper County, Virginia,
1781–1853](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:364176).
Its film table identifies Film 30927 and Image Group Number (DGS) 7578850,
with an index and a compiled register. The catalog note says the register
gives the marriage date, husband, wife, and minister; pages 101–117 may also
give parents' names when known.

## Focused results

FamilySearch Full-Text searches of DGS 7578850 for `Caton`, `Cayton`,
`Catton`, `Caten`, `Cation`, `Katon`, `Kitton`, `Kitten`, and `Kayton` were
run. The `Caton` search returned three
indexed results:

- The register image [3QS7-99XF-K342](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-99XF-K342?view=fullText&keywords=Caton&searchForm=advanced&lang=en)
  is image 39 of 121 and contains the 1847 entry for John C. Foushee and
  Mary E. Caton, dated 16 March 1847.
- The index image [3QSQ-G9XF-KSLC](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G9XF-KSLC?view=fullText&keywords=Caton&searchForm=advanced&lang=en)
  reads `Caton, Mary E., 37` and `Caton see also Kayton`.
- A second index-image result also surfaced, but no additional 1816–1819
  marriage entry was exposed.

The separate `Kayton` search returned only the index cross-references; it did
not return a register entry. The `Katon`, `Kitton`, and `Kitten` searches also
returned 0. The other tested spelling searches returned no result.

## Interpretation boundary

**Proof:** DGS 7578850 is an accessible Culpeper marriage index/register
compilation. Its current full-text index surfaces a Mary E. Caton marriage
in 1847 and a Caton/Kayton cross-reference, but no record-level 1816–1819
entry for the tested spellings.

**Inference only:** This is a bounded non-hit in FamilySearch's indexed
transcript layer, not proof that no 1816–1819 record exists. The underlying
LVA Reel 50 material remains the direct follow-up, especially because the LVA
guide notes that the original Marriage Register No. 1 was not filmed.

The Mary E. Caton entry is not connected to Aaron, Thomas Sr., or the
Fauquier Cayton candidate. No canonical claim is added.

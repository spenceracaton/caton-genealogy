# A7 — Fauquier deed index, 1833–1844: public route check

STATUS: blocked

## Question

Can a Fauquier deed or deed-index entry connect Thomas Caton, Aaron Caton, or
Moses Caton through a parent, sibling, or land conveyance in the years surrounding
the 1835–1840 migration?

## Record and access result

The Fauquier Circuit Court Clerk's public eSearch certified-dates page exposes
historic grantor-index image coverage from 1759–1913 and historic grantee-index
image coverage from 1759–1913. It does not expose a searchable Caton result or a
deed image in the accessible session. No Caton index page, deed book page, or
conveyance was retrieved.

The Library of Virginia's county guide independently identifies Fauquier Deed
Books 34–40 as covering 1833–1841, with the general grantor and grantee indexes
covering 1759–1914. These holdings define the next image target but do not supply
record-level evidence by themselves.

## Finding and limit

This is an access and holdings result only. It neither supports nor weakens a
Caton kinship or land hypothesis. A7 remains open for a readable historic index
image and the referenced deed page.

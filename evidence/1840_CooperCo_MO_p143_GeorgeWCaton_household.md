## Reading against the known family (C121, C123)
- M 30–40 = George W. (b. 1800; 39 on census day if born after 1 June). F 30–40 = Sarah (b. c.1807).
- M 5–10 = Nathan (b. Jan 1832). M 10–15 = one of George R. (b. c.1828) or Thomas N. (b. c.1830) — **both should sit in 10–15 and only one does**; the other is either mis-bracketed into 15–20 or absent.
- F under 5 ×2 = Sabina (b. c.1835) and one other girl.
- **Unexplained: M 15–20 ×2, M 20–30 ×2, F 15–20 ×2** — six people born c.1810–1825. None can be Sarah's children. Journeymen/apprentices and servants of a tailor's shop, or kin. Aaron (20 on 1 June 1840) fits a 20–30 tally; Joseph (16) fits a 15–20 tally.

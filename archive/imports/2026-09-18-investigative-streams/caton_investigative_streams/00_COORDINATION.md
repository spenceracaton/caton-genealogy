## Central corrections that every researcher must carry

**An age slot is not a named child.** The 1810/1820 transcription checks support the recorded counts, not the wife's identity, sons, grandchildren, or a mandatory remarriage. The available 1830 transcription omits p. 471. [N2]

**The copied consent is not an autograph.** It genuinely prints Thomas Caton Jr. as William's consenting father, but cannot identify handwriting or automatically equate him with 1830 Thomas Jun. A confirmed 20-30 census age would break that particular adult father/son merger, not identify the correct older man. [N1]

**The older Boonville tailor is not yet the Virginia patriarch or necessarily distinct from the 1828 groom.** The 77-year-old transcribed age conflicts with the reported 1810/1830 Fauquier ages. The earlier conversational claim that the groom had a convincingly independent Missouri trail was too strong. Compiled child lists and militia name matches are insufficient to resolve those identities. [N3,N5]

**The older Aaron is not proved childless or dead before 1820.** A childless-looking1810 household does not decide later children; the exact 1825 death date is unverified. [N5]

**A pension application has not been proved from the disputed index card.** C184's inference must be tested against the card, not repeated as fact. [R2:C184]

**The geographic route is person-specific.** Daniel Laurie's Ohio-born child does not prove that Thomas, William and Aaron all migrated together. The 1835 departure of every Thomas is not established by the known marriage and family history alone. A 1840 index negative cannot locate a 1835 stop. [R2:C098,C126,C189,C196]

**Joseph's brotherhood and birthplace need original testimony.** Keep the niece report, any uncle-by-marriage alternative, and Fairfax/Fauquier readings distinct. The pending-request status must come from a current check, not an old handoff. Joseph's Saline probate remains parked. [R2:C110,C118,C120]

## Definition of progress

Progress is a newly acquired record, a demonstrated identity link, a contradicted hypothesis, a precise validated source locator, or a genuinely bounded negative. Re-reading the same transcription is not a new discovery. A third account copying the same genealogy is not independent corroboration. A full-text or index failure is not proof of record absence.

Use the repository's A/B/C framework but grade source observations separately from reasoning. A photographed typescript can be directly observed while its copied relationship is still derivative. A source gradedA can contain mistaken information; a persuasive argument can rest on incomplete coverage. Every inference must say what would materially weaken it.

For a future parentage conclusion, assemble an identity-correct chain of evidence for Aaron and each proposed parent. An explicit parent statement still needs source/informant assessment. A well-supported indirect proof may also be possible, but it must explain the relevant conflicts and alternatives rather than merely count coincidences. The goal is not to choose the most attractive story.

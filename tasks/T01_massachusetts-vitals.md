# T01 — Massachusetts vitals, c.1842–1844

**Priority: 1. The most promising unexplored avenue, and it survived a challenge.**

Aaron's 1850 Delaware County, Ohio household (claim C006) contains **Julia Caton,
age 7, born Massachusetts, attending school**. Aaron married in Ohio in Aug 1841;
his next child, **Mary E., 5, was born in Ohio c.1845**. So the family was in
Massachusetts for a window of roughly **1842–1844** and back in Ohio by c.1845.

> A parallel research thread on 10 Sep 2026 read the census image as "Julia, age 1,
> birthplace appears to read Missouri" and proposed it would rewrite the migration
> timeline. The USGenWeb proofread transcription of p.179a does not support that
> reading. Julia is 7, Massachusetts, in school. **This task stands.**

**Target:** the birth record of **Julia Caton, c.1843**, in the Massachusetts
statewide vital records. Registration began 1841, so it should exist.

**Why it outranks everything else on the Aaron side:** a Massachusetts birth record
names **both parents** and usually **the father's birthplace**. It will not name
Aaron's parents, but it is the best available independent statement of where Aaron
himself was born — which currently rests on the 1850 census and on later report.

**Secondary:** city directories for a tailor named Caton, **1842–1844**, in Boston,
Lowell, Lawrence, Worcester, Springfield, New Bedford. A tailor in a mill town is
findable.

**Also check:** a Massachusetts marriage or death record for any Caton in the same
window, and whether Sarah Ann (Gardner) Caton has Massachusetts kin — the family
went there for a reason, and a Gardner connection is the likeliest one.

**Surname variants:** Caton, Catton, Caten, Cation, Eaton, Cason, Kitten.

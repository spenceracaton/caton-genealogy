(1) Alexandria Gazette & Daily Advertiser, 13 July 1820, p.2 (sn83026170/1820-07-13/ed-1/?sp=2); repeated 17, 31 July and 1-17 Aug 1820:
  "A CAMP MEETING will be held on the land of Geo. W. Lane, adjoining the town of Centreville, in the county of Fairfax, Va., to commence on the 16th of August next.
   As the primary object of these meetings is sometimes in a degree defeated from the want of proper order, considerable pains have been taken to secure the preservation of good order and management on the present occasion: to this end, the inhabitants of Centreville and its vicinity, particularly the owners of the land adjacent thereto, with the magistrates and other civil officers, [have signed the pledge] which we here insert to discourage hucksters and venders of spirituous liquors ...
   We, the subscribers, being desirous to obtain a camp meeting in our neighborhood, and feeling disposed to aid in the preservation of order and good management of the same, do hereby cordially and solemnly pledge ourselves to the ministers and managers of the meeting, to discountenance all disorder and improper conduct, and to prevent (as far as we may individually have the power) the sale of spirituous liquors or the settling of hucksters on the lots or land in the neighborhood of the meeting, over which any of us may have control.
   Alexander Waugh; Geo. N. Buckley; Enoch Grigsby; Jno. Bronaugh; Ths. Swayne; Elisha Janny; B. Harrison; Wm. Lane; Geo. W. Lane; Richard Williams; Amos Hoff; Wormely Carter; Henry Whaley; Fewel A. Perry; John D. Perry; William Carr Lane; John Shud; Henry Hobb; B. M. Lane; MOSES CATON; Jas. H. Hooe; Jno. Brown; Jas. Gaines; James L. Triplett; John Hening; Jas. M. Roberdeau; Jeremiah Hutchinson; Wm. Larkin; Coleman Lewis.
   N.B. ... CHRISTOPHER FREGE, RICHD. McALLISTER. July 13."

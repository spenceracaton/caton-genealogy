# S09 — Aaron Caton federal files, authenticated Fold3 and Ancestry review

STREAM: S09 — Aaron Caton federal military and pension records
STATUS: found — complete CMSR read; pension application itself not located
QUESTION TESTED: Do Aaron Caton's authenticated federal military or pension records state an exact Virginia birthplace, pre-1841 residence, parent, sibling, or early acquaintance?
EXECUTION: 24 September 2026
ACCESS: Authenticated Fold3 and Ancestry image viewers. No order, trial, purchase, form submission, or archive contact was made. No subscription-restricted image was retained in the public repository.

## Result

The proof test produced one material result. Aaron's original veteran enlistment paper, signed `Aaron Caton` on 5 January 1864, states that he was **born in Fauquier County, Virginia**, was aged forty-four, and was a tailor. It describes him as having hazel eyes, brown hair, a dark complexion, and standing five feet eight inches. This is the first located record naming an exact Virginia county for the direct-line Aaron.

The county statement materially strengthens the Fauquier origin model and weakens Wheeling/Ohio County as the working birthplace model. It does **not** name Aaron's parents, siblings, childhood household, or a person who knew him before 1841. It therefore changes the geographic analysis without proving the proposed relationship to either Fauquier Thomas Caton household.

The authenticated Fold3 filmstrip contains **37 Aaron images, pages 1–37**. Page 37 is the final Aaron image; the next-image control opens `Caton, William B - Page 1`, image 200446922. This corrects the earlier public-result description of a 38-image Aaron jacket. The browse label `Caton, Aaron (44)` is consistent with the age on the veteran enlistment paper; it is not an image count.

The pension application packet was not found online and remains unread. Three authenticated federal indexes identify the same invalid application 233783, but two index series disagree on the filing year:

- T288, the General Index card on Ancestry, reads `1876 April 10`.
- T289, the Organization Index card on Fold3, reads `1877 Apl. 10`.
- A1158, the Pension Numerical Index card, confirms invalid application 233783 for `Caten, Aaron`, `E 2 Mo. Cav.`, without resolving the filing-date conflict.

The T288 image corrects the repository's present `12 Apr. 76` reading to **10 Apr. 1876**. T288 and T289 both leave widow, minor, certificate, and filing-state fields blank. Those blanks do not prove that no separate dependent claim ever existed.

## Pre-registered proof test

Before opening the jacket, the material-change test was: an exact Virginia birthplace, pre-1841 residence, parent or sibling, or a witness explicitly stating where and when the witness knew Aaron before 1841. Service-only muster facts were to carry identity weight but no parentage weight; application 233783 was not to be described without reading it.

Outcome:

- **Exact birthplace:** hit — Fauquier County, Virginia, stated by Aaron on the 5 January 1864 veteran enlistment paper.
- **Pre-1841 residence:** no statement located in the 37 CMSR images.
- **Parent or sibling:** no statement located.
- **Pre-1841 witness:** no witness testimony or such statement located.
- **Application 233783:** not located as an online packet and not read.

## Identity assessment

IDENTITY MATCH: ACCEPTED, high confidence.

Evidence for:

- all three pension indexes carry the same rare combination `Caten, Aaron`, invalid application 233783, Company E, 2nd Missouri Cavalry;
- the 37-image CMSR jacket is `Aaron Caton`, Company E, 2nd Missouri Cavalry;
- the original 1864 paper is signed `Aaron Caton`, gives age forty-four, occupation tailor, and Fauquier County, Virginia, birth;
- age about 43–44 in January 1864 is consistent within ordinary nineteenth-century age error with the independently reported 16 May 1820 birth;
- tailor is the direct-line Aaron's independently documented occupation;
- Chillicothe enrollment, veteran reenlistment, and final muster-out match the retained state service abstract.

Evidence against or unresolved:

- the pension surname is spelled `Caten` rather than `Caton`;
- application 233783 remains unread;
- T288 and T289 disagree by one year on the filing date;
- the 1861 joining date is 13 August in the federal CMSR but 15 August in the retained state service abstract;
- no pension index gives residence, spouse, age, or certificate number.

No competing Company E, 2nd Missouri Cavalry Aaron was located. The original signed enlistment paper removes any practical identity doubt about the CMSR. The pension-card identity remains an inference until the application is read, but it is stronger than before because the authenticated organization and numerical indexes independently agree on name, unit, class, and application number.

## Conflicts and corrections

1. **G020, G013, T04, and the prior THREAD 5 note:** the T288 image reads `1876 April 10`, not `12 Apr. 76`. The same image independently confirms `E. 2 Mo. Cav.` and invalid application 233783.
2. **T288 versus T289:** T288 reads 10 April 1876; T289 reads 10 April 1877. Preserve both readings. Do not silently normalize T289 to T288.
3. **Prior 38-image description:** authenticated sequential navigation and the filmstrip show Aaron pages 1–37; the next file is William B. Caton. The earlier count was not a content-level inspection.
4. **C023:** the federal company muster-in card says joined 13 August 1861 at Chillicothe; the retained state abstract says 15 August. The original 1864 veteran enlistment states age forty-four in January 1864, which implies birth about 1819–1820, not C023's `c.1817` reading if the age belongs to that reenlistment event.
5. **Application contents:** no disability diagnosis, pension declaration, medical examination, witness affidavit, rejection rationale, or family statement from application 233783 was seen. The two CMSR sickness notations cannot be represented as the pension allegation.

## Parentage effect

The self-reported Fauquier County birthplace is direct, original evidence for county of birth. It materially strengthens the Fauquier/Prince William model over the Wheeling/Ohio County model and removes `Virginia only` as the ceiling of Aaron's known birthplace evidence.

It does not identify which Fauquier household held him, prove that elder Thomas or Moses was his father, identify his mother, or establish Joseph as a brother. It also does not explain Aaron's absence from the known Caton households in 1830. The parentage case remains unproved; the new fact sharpens the next Fauquier tests rather than completing them.

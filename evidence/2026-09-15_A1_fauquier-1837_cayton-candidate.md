# A1 — Fauquier personal-property tax, 1837: `Cayton` candidate

## Question

Does the Fauquier personal-property tax list show Thomas Caton carrying Aaron
and Joseph as additional tithables in the years when they reached the taxable
age?

## Retrieval

The authenticated FamilySearch session showed `Account: Spencer5362`. The
FamilySearch Catalog record is [Personal property tax lists,
1782–1850](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:775969), filmed
from Virginia State Library originals. Its 1833–1845 item is:

- Film 2024534; Image Group Number (DGS) 007849111.
- Title: `Personal Property Tax Records, 1833–1845, Fauquier County,
  Virginia`.
- The catalog specifies `(no index)` and the image viewer exposes 717 images.
- [Image 263 of 717](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQK-TS8S-R?view=explore)
  is in the 1837 section of the film sequence. The adjoining 1837 section
  heading is visible in the film's earlier pages; the candidate page itself
  is a handwritten taxpayer page dated in April/May sequence.
- The viewer's image identifier for image 263 is `TH-909-58196-63985-52`;
  image 258 carries the visible section label `1837-B`.

## Record-level observation

High-resolution image 263 contains three rows whose surname is visibly written
`Cayton`, not `Caton`:

- `Cayton Thomas`
- `Cayton William`
- `Cayton Thos.`

Each of these rows has `1` in the first numeric field, followed by ditto marks
or blanks in the other visible fields. The row names and the numeric entries
are preserved in the [full page image](2026-09-15_A1_fauquier_1837_image263.jpg)
and the [focused handwritten crop](2026-09-15_A1_fauquier_1837_image263_right_crop.jpg).

The page's FamilySearch citation identifies it as image 263 of 717 in DGS
007849111. The image viewer metadata describes the volume as personal-property
tax records, but does not supply an index or a normalized surname.

No brace, indentation, or explicit household notation is visible linking the
adjacent Cayton lines, and the headings for the numeric columns are not
visible on this page. The tax-column meaning therefore remains unresolved.

## Interpretation boundary

**Proof:** The 1833–1845 Fauquier tax image set is accessible in the
authenticated session, and image 263 contains the three handwritten `Cayton`
rows described above.

**Inference only:** `Cayton` could be a spelling variant worth testing against
the Prince William–Fauquier Caton cluster. The image does not identify these
people as Catons, does not identify their relationships, and does not name
Aaron or Joseph. The first numeric field is reported without assigning a
column meaning beyond what is visibly written.

The authenticated transcript candidate sweep found no `Caton` or `Cayton`
string in the 1836, 1838, or 1839 sections. That is only a locator result:
FamilySearch's AI transcript can miss handwritten names and is not negative
proof. Only the 1837 candidate page was visually verified here.

## Finding and limit

This is a record-level spelling-variant lead, not a parentage result. It does
not establish that Thomas Caton carried two or three tithables and does not
justify a canonical claim. A1 remains open for manual image review of the
remaining target-year surname bands and for an independent identification of
the Fauquier `Cayton` family.

## Significance

1. **George W. Caton** explicitly called "the tailor" among early Boonville tradesmen.
   Confirms C121–C123 (George W. Caton, tailor, b. 1800 Alexandria, Boonville
   1832–49, d. 1865).

2. **Joseph Caton** named as one of the first tailors in a Cooper County township —
   almost certainly Joseph Caton (b. 1823, Fauquier), the tailor known from census
   records (C076). Both George W. and Joseph were tailors in Cooper County.

3. The 1883 county history does not group George W. and Joseph as relatives — they
   appear in different township sections. George W. Caton was from Alexandria (C121);
   Joseph from Fauquier (C076). They were likely master/apprentice or professional
   peers, not necessarily kin.

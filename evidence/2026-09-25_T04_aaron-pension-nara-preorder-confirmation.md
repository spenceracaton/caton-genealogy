## Current complete-file product and fee

Use the full-file product, not the selected-document packet:

**Full Pension Application File — Civil War and Later**, identified in NARA's online ordering materials as **Federal Military Pension Application — Civil War and Later Complete File (`NATF 85D`)**.

Current official terms:

- $80 for the first 100 pages.
- $0.70 for each page after 100; NARA provides a quote.
- The complete-file category includes the Pension Documents Packet.
- If a file exceeds 100 pages, the NATF 85 instructions state that NARA sends the first block of pages and a quote for the remainder. Selecting the complete-file product therefore does not by itself guarantee receipt of every page for $80; completeness requires accepting and paying any additional-page quote.
- The $30 Pension Documents Packet is limited to as many as eight specified genealogical documents found in the file. It is unsuitable because it can omit affidavits, depositions, medical records, correspondence, and the decision or rejection rationale.

## Can a no-cost location confirmation precede an order?

Yes, as a reference inquiry, with one limitation.

NARA directs questions about pre-World War I Army pension files to `archives1reference@nara.gov` and says to allow 10–12 business days. Archives 1's researcher guidance says its staff can help determine whether a visit is needed or whether another way to obtain copies is available. Sending the inquiry below does not itself place an order or authorize a fee.

NARA's public guidance does not promise that reference staff will pull and verify a specific `SO` file before an order. The inquiry can request that confirmation; the staff response will determine whether they will provide it without a reproduction order.

The old NATF 85 form's “There is no charge for an unsuccessful search” is narrower. That form says that if NARA locates the identified file, it will make the selected copies. An NATF 85 submission is therefore an order, not a free successful-location check with a later purchase decision. Do not submit NATF 85 merely to confirm that the file exists.

Sources: [NARA pre-World War I pension guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/army/pensions); [Archives 1 researcher information](https://www.archives.gov/dc/researcher-info); [Archives 1 textual-records guidance](https://www.archives.gov/dc/research/textual-records); [NARA obtain-copies guidance](https://www.archives.gov/research/order).

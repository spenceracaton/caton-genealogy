# T22 — Statewide census screen and Culpeper public-source checks

## Research questions

1. Does a statewide 1820 or 1830 Virginia census search surface another Caton
   household that could explain Aaron or Joseph outside the already-read
   Fauquier/Fairfax cluster?
2. Do the publicly searchable Culpeper will abstract or marriage indexes recover
   a Caton entry missed by the FamilySearch indexed-layer checks?

## Sources and retrieval

### FamilySearch census index

- [United States, Census, 1820](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1803955), authenticated search with place restricted to Virginia and the surname set to exact `Caton`.
- [United States, Census, 1830](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1803958), authenticated search with place restricted to Virginia and the surname set to exact `Caton`.
- The 1820 exact `Caton` search returned four indexed heads:
  - Moses Caton, Fairfax;
  - Thomas Caton, Princess Anne;
  - James Caton, Chesterfield;
  - Thos. Caton, Fauquier.
- The 1830 exact `Caton` search returned ten indexed entries, covering the
  already-known Fauquier/Fairfax/Ohio County cluster plus Henry Caton in
  Chesterfield, Isaac Caton in Kanawha, and John Caton in Preston. The list
  contained no Aaron Caton or Joseph Caton head.
- Exact 1820 spelling-variant checks returned: `Cayton` two heads (Brooke and
  Henry), `Catton` two (Monongalia and Petersburg), `Caten` two (Richmond and
  Princess Anne), `Katon` zero, and `Kitten` one (Joseph Kitten, Prince William).
  The `Kitten` result is not a Caton result and was not merged.

### Public Culpeper sources

- The [FamilySearch digital volume *Culpeper County, Virginia, will abstracts*](https://www.familysearch.org/library/books/records/item/469225-culpeper-county-virginia-will-abstracts), identifier 2536133, is available online and has 221 viewer pages. Its in-viewer searches returned zero results for `Caton`, `Cayton`, `Catton`, `Katon`, and `Aaron`.
- The public [Genealogy Trails Culpeper marriage index](https://genealogytrails.com/vir/culpeper/marriages.html), headed “Marriage Record Index, 1781 to 1825,” was read for `Caton`, `Cayton`, `Caten`, and `Kitton`; no target entry surfaced.
- The public-domain scan of Raleigh Travers Green's [*Genealogical and Historical Notes on Culpeper County, Virginia*](https://archive.org/details/genealogicalhist00gree) states that the following marriage record is complete from 1781 to 1 January 1825. Visual review of the printed C-section on scan pages 213–215 (printed pages 59–61) found no `Caton`, `Cayton`, `Catton`, `Caten`, `Katon`, `Kitton`, or `Kitten` entry. This compilation records the year only and is a secondary source; it does not replace the Reel 50 images.
- The [Library of Virginia Culpeper guide](https://www.lva.virginia.gov/collections/ccmf/VA/VA067) still identifies Reel 50 as the compiled 1781–1853 marriage index/register route, while noting that Marriage Register No. 1 was not filmed. The public checks do not replace that direct request.

## Findings and limits

The statewide census search is a bounded FamilySearch-index screen, not a
manual reading of every Virginia census image. It recovers no additional
candidate father for Aaron or Joseph and adds no parentage weight. It supports
the existing conclusion that the relevant 1820–1830 Virginia search space is
the known Fauquier/Fairfax group, while preserving the possibility of an index
error or an unindexed/misread page.

The public Culpeper will-abstract and marriage-index checks produced no Caton
entry. Green's printed 1781–1825 marriage list independently contains no visible
Caton variant in the C-section, but it is still a secondary compilation and records
only the year. These are secondary/indexed non-hits only; they do not close the
direct LVA Reel 50 route or the unfilmed Marriage Register No. 1 gap.

## Status

- Statewide 1820/1830 census index screen: bounded.
- Public Culpeper abstract/index screen: bounded.
- Direct Culpeper 1816–1819 marriage sequence: still open; highest-value next
  direct request.
- Grade: C for the search boundaries; no parentage weight.

## Related disambiguation

FamilySearch also surfaced a 28 June 1809 `Thomas Caton`–`Mary Balentine`
marriage record. Opening the original shows the source title **Marriage Bonds,
1808–1810, Norfolk County, Virginia**, image 58 of 121—not Fauquier. The
record is attached by a volunteer to a Family Tree person named Thomas William
Caton, 1767–1863; that attachment is not proof of identity. The record therefore
must not be used as the older Fauquier Thomas's first marriage or as evidence
for Aaron's parentage.

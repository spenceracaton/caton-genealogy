Crop-first inspection of the candidate scan at leaf 293 shows a handwritten `D. Laurie` entry and multiple nearby Caton entries, including rows read as `Wm Caton`. The candidate image is available at:

`https://ia903205.us.archive.org/BookReader/BookReaderImages.php?zip=/8/items/populationsc18400222unit/populationsc18400222unit_jp2.zip&file=populationsc18400222unit_jp2/populationsc18400222unit_0293.jp2&id=populationsc18400222unit&scale=4&rotate=0`

The image was not sufficient, in this pass, to securely read the full household structure or identify an older male as Thomas Sr. It is cluster corroboration only, not proof of Aaron’s parentage and not a new canonical claim.

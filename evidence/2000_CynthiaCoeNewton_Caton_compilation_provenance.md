# Cynthia Coe Newton / 2000 Caton compilation — provenance memo

**Status:** partial. Research-only note; no canonical files changed.

## Lead and public-source chain

The reported 2000 compilation is not discoverable as a digitized standalone file by exact-name or exact-fact searches. A likely related Winfield publication is documented in the National Register nomination for the Pettit Cleaners Building:

- Don Potter, *The Caton Legacy in Winfield: A Driving Tour of Historic Winfield, Kansas* (Winfield Convention and Tourism Committee, 2000).
- Stable catalog-like citation: National Park Service, NRHP nomination, Pettit Cleaners Building, Section 9, p. 1: https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/04001427_text
- Exact wording in the bibliography: `Potter, Don, AIA, Winfield Convention and Tourism Committee, The Caton Legacy in Winfield: A Driving Tour of Historic Winfield, Kansas, 2000.`
- The same nomination says the biographical chain is based on assorted family photographs, Connelley's 1928 *History of Kansas State and People*, the 1948 *Winfield Daily Courier*, city directories, and related local sources. It gives William B. Caton's arrival in Winfield in 1878, his 1880 marble works, and Harry Aaron Caton's parentage, but **does not state Aaron Caton's birth date/place or parents**.

The Cowley County Historical Society Museum has an online “Caton Family / Will B. Caton” page:

- https://www.cchsm.com/resources/misc/wortman_cc/caton_will_b.html
- Exact wording: `Mr. Caton was born in Delaware, Ohio. He served his country in the Civil war for nearly four years as a bugler in the Second Missouri Cavalry, Merrill’s Horse...`
- Exact wording: `In 1869 Mr. Caton was married to Miss Julia Blankenmeister... He came to Winfield in 1879...`
- Exact wording: `Mr. and Mrs. Caton had four children: Mrs. Lottie Caton Abbott, Louis M. Caton, proprietor of the Caton dairy south of town, Mrs. Frank Root and Harry A. Caton.`

The museum states that its Frankie Cullison Research Collection includes “files on individuals,” genealogical records, oral histories, scrapbooks, photographs, and related materials: https://www.cchsm.com/resources/ . Its contact details are 1011 Mannsfield Street, Winfield, KS 67156; 620-221-4811; museumcchsm@gmail.com.

A later derivative article, “W.B. Caton, Caton Marble Works, Winfield Kansas” (Midwest Death Trip, 20 Aug. 2024), explicitly credits Cynthia Newton for several Caton family photographs:

- https://midwesterndeathtrip.com/w-b-caton-caton-marble-works-winfield-kansas/
- Search-result exact wording: `Image W. B. Caton, 1882, photo courtesy Cynthia Newton, enhanced.`
- It also says `Image Harry Aaron Caton, date unknown, photo courtesy Cynthia Newton` and credits Cynthia Newton for the approximately-1900 Caton family-home photograph and the photograph of Christine Caton with the lions.
- The article repeats the family tradition that William B. Caton was born in Delaware County, Ohio, moved to Missouri, and came to Winfield in 1878/79. It is derivative and provides no source for Aaron's 1820 birth claim.

The Cowley County Heritage Book material preserves a related family-history provenance chain. The Caton article says it was written by Lottie Caton Abbot, found by her great-great niece Mary Carolyn Love among materials Caroline Louise Crapster Caton kept, and submitted by Barbara Caton Love: https://www.ksgenweb.org/KSCowley/people/pages/pg136-140.html . Exact wording: `It was through these pieces of history that we keep alive our past and know our families.` This is useful for locating family papers, but the displayed article does not give Aaron's birth citation or parents.

## What was not located

- No public scan, library record, or searchable catalog entry for a Cynthia Coe Newton family-tree compilation containing the exact assertions “Aaron Caton born 16 May 1820,” “Wheeling, West Virginia,” “Virginia-born tailor,” “married Sarah Ann Gardner,” or “father of William Bramble.”
- No author note, footnote, source list, original certificate, Bible page, letter, obituary, or other primary record publicly tied to those exact Aaron assertions.
- No public source found that names Aaron Caton's parents. The Winfield sources establish the later William B. Caton family and preserve photographs/family-history materials, not Aaron's parentage.

## Retrieval request

Request the Cowley County Historical Society Museum to search the Frankie Cullison Research Collection and Caton family files for:

1. Don Potter, *The Caton Legacy in Winfield: A Driving Tour of Historic Winfield, Kansas* (2000), including any unpublished draft, source list, research notes, or accompanying family tree.
2. Any item labeled Cynthia Coe Newton, Cynthia Newton, Caton family genealogy, Caton family tree, William B. Caton papers, Julia Blankenmeister Caton papers, Lottie Caton Abbot, Caroline Louise Crapster Caton, Mary Carolyn Love, or Barbara Caton Love.
3. The original source and exact page/image for the statements that Aaron Caton was born 16 May 1820 at Wheeling, Virginia/West Virginia; was a Virginia-born tailor; married Sarah Ann Gardner; and was William B. Caton's father.
4. Any explicit statement of Aaron Caton's parents, with a scan or full archival citation.
5. Provenance for the photographs credited to Cynthia Newton, including donor name, accession number, captions, reverse inscriptions, and whether the donor supplied a genealogy or oral-history interview.

Ask for collection/folder/item identifiers even if the material is inaccessible, and ask whether staff can provide a scan or supervised reading of the relevant pages. Separately ask Kansas Historical Society to check its Archives Catalog and manuscript/photo collections for the same names and for the Julia Caton autobiography cited in the Pettit Cleaners nomination; request the exact collection number, box/folder, and any restriction or duplication fee.

## Assessment

The Winfield/Cowley trail is a real provenance lead for the later Caton family and may identify the custodian or donor of the compilation. It does **not** currently upgrade the Aaron birth date/place or parentage above lead status. The exact request must target the underlying family papers and source citations, not the derivative web article.

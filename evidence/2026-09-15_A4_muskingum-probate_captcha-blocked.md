# A4: Moses Caton Muskingum probate search remains CAPTCHA blocked

## Research question

Test A4 in `PARENTAGE-TESTS.md` asks whether Moses B. Caton's Muskingum County estate, opened in 1839, names heirs or relationships that can distinguish Aaron's parentage hypotheses.

## Source and retrieval

- Muskingum County Probate Court: <https://www.muskingumcountyoh.gov/Courts/Probate/>
- The court states that will and estate records are available from 1804 to the present and links its public record-search portal.
- The portal was reached at `http://74.219.84.227:8022/recordSearch.php?searchForm=6003`.
- The portal requires acceptance of its terms and presents a CAPTCHA before the search form will return records. The intended search was for `Moses Caton`, with an approximate 1839–1845 filing window and estate case type.
- SAMPUBCO's public Muskingum will-book testator index was checked as a secondary control. It lists `George W. Caton` at `OH-59-D-553` but has no `Moses Caton` entry. The index covers wills, not all estate or administration files.

## Finding boundary

No probate docket, case number, will, administration record, heir list, or image was retrieved. The CAPTCHA boundary is an access failure, not a negative search result. The public court page also directs researchers to provide exact names and approximate dates rather than treating an unperformed search as proof of absence.

The SAMPUBCO result is a bounded negative only for the visible will-book testator index. It does not exclude an intestate Moses estate, an administration docket, or a file indexed under a variant spelling.

# Ohio County / Wheeling test — Aaron Caton

**Research date:** 17 September 2026  
**Scope:** Research only. No canonical files changed.

## Claim provenance

- The active brief identifies “Wheeling” as unsourced and cites C075: every located record gives Aaron's birthplace only as Virginia; no original record naming Wheeling has been found.
- The earliest dated project artifact that states the claim is the archived living handoff, updated 9 September 2026: “Born 16 May 1820; later sources report Wheeling, Virginia.” It names no later source.
- The next archived research memo is more explicit but still not source-level: “Compiled trees give Aaron's birthplace as Wheeling.” It also states: “Reported, unverified. Aaron's dates of 16 May 1820 ... come from compiled family trees.” No tree owner, title, URL, image, or copied wording is preserved. Therefore the earliest traceable source is a compiled-tree assertion recorded in the project in September 2026, not an identified original record.

## Direct evidence located

1. **1830 federal census, Ohio County, Virginia, p. 209, NARA M19 roll 198.** Original page image retained as `evidence/OhioCounty1830_p209_names.jpg`. The page reads one household headed **Thomas Caton**: one male aged 5–10, one male aged 30–40, one female under 5, and one female aged 20–30. This is a primary household enumeration, not a relationship statement. It does not name Aaron.

2. **1820 federal census, Ohio County, Virginia.** C075 records the bounded negative that no Caton household was located in the county index/image search. This is a negative census result, not proof that Aaron was absent from the county or that Wheeling was false.

3. **Ohio County marriage index, 1834–1850.** The repository's T06 search of the official West Virginia Vital Research Records index found no John Boring marriage under Boring/Boren/Boreing/Borring/Borin. This does not identify Aaron and does not test the Wheeling birth claim directly.

4. **Indexed Wheeling church material, 1785–1954.** C091 records a bounded negative for Caton/Caten in the searched FamilySearch church-record image group. The result is an index/image-group search boundary, not a county-wide negative and not proof against a birth in 1820.

## Ohio County record-series coverage found

The West Virginia and Regional History Center's Ohio County microfilm listing identifies the following relevant series:

- **Will Book #1, 1810–1830**; additional will books/abstracts covering 1810–1829 and 1824–1841.
- **Deed Books 9–10, 1816–1820**; deed indexes covering 1795–1828 and 1811–1830.
- **Land tax, 1813–1822**; land books 1830–1859.
- **Guardian Bond #430, 1797–1866.**
- **Marriages, 1790–1827.**
- **Record Book #558, 1816.**
- Public survey coverage separately lists **Order Book 1818–1825**, **Order Book 1825–1830**, and **County Court Minutes 1818–1825**.
- The West Virginia State Archives' public county-records page says: **“Ohio County — Circuit Clerk - being processed.”**

These are verified access/coverage leads. No page image or entry naming Aaron, a Caton parent, or an apprenticeship/poor-relief relationship was retrieved in this pass.

## Result

**Wheeling birth: unverified, grade C.** The claim remains a compiled-tree tradition. Ohio County contains a real Caton household by 1830 and an adult Caton marriage presence in 1818, but neither record connects to Aaron. The 1830 Thomas Caton household is a lead only; do not infer parentage from surname, age fit, or proximity.

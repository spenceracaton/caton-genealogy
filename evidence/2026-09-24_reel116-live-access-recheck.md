## Exact current retrieval route and limits

### Full review: interlibrary loan

The best exact route remains a request by a participating local public or
university library for LVA Fauquier County Reel 116. The borrowing library may
submit through OCLC or an ALA interlibrary-loan form. The LVA policy currently
states:

- 49-day loan;
- one renewal for 28 additional days, except overdue items or items on hold;
- five-reel limit per patron;
- no LVA charge for the loan or copies, although the borrowing library may
  impose a local fee or shorter use period.

The borrowing library must confirm that it accepts microfilm, has a
reader/scanner, permits saved scans, and whether use is restricted to its
building. No request was submitted.

### Bounded first inspection: Virtual Reading Room

LVA's Virtual Reading Room remains a free remote route for one reel of
microfilm in a one-hour Zoom session. If the reel is not finished, another
appointment is required. A researcher may schedule up to three appointments
per month and may select up to ten pages for staff photographs after a session.
The session is not recorded, is not a research consultation, and image quality
is limited by Zoom. Appointments must be booked by the Thursday before the
requested date and may be scheduled up to one month ahead.

LVA's site currently announces a Public Services closure for 21–26 September
2026. The Virtual Reading Room page states that appointments are unavailable
when the library is closed. No appointment was scheduled.

This route is suitable for finding the volume title, internal year breaks,
page numbering, and a small number of target entries. It is not sufficient for
a dependable page-by-page read of thirteen years in one appointment.

Six persons. **No male over 26.** One person in manufactures (tailors and printers were both counted as manufactures in 1820).

## Reading
- Michael Caton is himself one of the two males 16–26 (b. 1794–1804), or the tally is short a bracket. The 1822 and 1827 Washington directories list **Michael Caton, printer**, n. side E St. north between 6th and 7th west.
- George W. Caton, b. 1800, was 20 on census day (7 Aug 1820) and fits the second male 16–26. Unnamed.
- **No male under 10**: Aaron (b. 16 May 1820) is not in this household. If Aaron is George W.'s son, he was not with George in Washington in 1820.
- The woman 45+ (b. before 1775) is the right age to be the mother of men born 1794–1804; the girls under 10, 10–16 and the woman 16–26 are sisters, a wife, or boarders.
- No male 45+: the Irish Revolutionary soldier (C121) is not here either.

## Observations (not conclusions)

- **p.148, City of Boonville, dwelling/family 66, line 24: Thomas Caton, 77, M, "Taylor", b. Virginia**, the last
  member of John P. Maddex's household (Maddex 47, constable, b. Virginia; wife Parthema G. 39 b. Virginia;
  Jane 17 b. Virginia; three children b. Missouri). Age 77 on 14 Aug 1850 = b. c.1772–73. No relationship column
  exists in 1850; the placement at the foot of the Maddex household says nothing about kinship.
- **p.184, District 23, dwelling 335: James Caton, 19, carpenter, b. Virginia**, in John C. Howard's household
  (Howards b. Virginia). A Virginia-born Caton b. c.1831 not previously noted in the project.
- **p.186, District 23, dwelling 357: William Caton, 38, farmer, b. Virginia; John W. Caton, 18, laborer, b.
  Virginia; Aaron F. 8, Sarah Ann 6, Elizabeth 4, Wingfield 2, all b. Missouri.** John W., b. c.1832 in
  Virginia, could be a son of William and Ann F. (m. Jan 1829) born before the move, or a younger kinsman;
  the 1865 partition (C149) names William's heirs as Joseph, Aaron F., Sarah A. and Daniel R. only, so if he was
  a son he died or was omitted before 1865. No wife is transcribed on this page; the page break follows line
  42. The project's existing 1850 Cooper reading (the Laurie-neighbourhood page) uses a different page
  numbering; reconcile before citing either.
- **dwelling 902: John Caton, 53, farmer, $2,000, b. Delaware**, with Columbus M. 19, Benjamin 13,
  Elizabeth 11, Ophelia 9 (Missouri) — the Delaware-origin Cooper Caton line already separated in C096.
- **dwelling 1038: Thomas Caton, 40, farmer, $300, b. Kentucky**, wife 40 b. Kentucky, Lucinda 18, James 16 (Kentucky),
  Newton 14, Nimrod 12, Nancy 6, infant 1 (Missouri) — the Kentucky Thomas, distinct from the 77-year-old.
- Nothing in this transcription places the direct-line Aaron (b. 1820) in Cooper County in 1850; he is in
  Delaware Co., Ohio that year (C005–C006).

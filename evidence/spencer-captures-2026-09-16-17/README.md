# Spencer's FamilySearch captures, 16–17 Sep 2026 — imported 18 Sep 2026

Raw image downloads from Spencer's signed-in FamilySearch session on 16 Sep (21:19–21:20) and
17 Sep 2026 (11:58–18:52), found in `~/Downloads` and not previously in the repository. Filenames
are the browser's (`image_N.jpg` = FamilySearch "download image", N as in `image (N).jpg`;
`DGS_IMAGE.jpeg` = viewer download with image-group and image number). Four of the 17 Sep images
were promoted to named files in `evidence/` (see the bottom of this file); 42 others were already
present as the Codex tax packet (`tax-departure-2026-09-17/`) or the 15 Sep T18 crops and were not
re-imported.

Identifications below are from a single low-resolution look on 18 Sep by Claude, for the purpose of
naming what is here. They are **not transcriptions** and carry no grade. Year attributions marked
"c." are inferred from adjacent names and form layout, not from a heading on the image. Everything
here needs a heading image and a column-by-column read before it is cited.

# T08 — The England set. All blocked on paid access or a records request.

Grouped because they share one blocker: none can be done from free sources.
Do not send an agent at these expecting a retrieval. Send one to **price and
route** them, or place the records requests.

Ranked by how much each would move join 1 or join 3:

1. **Norfolk baptisms 1640–1670**, every Theophilus Kitton / Kitten / Ketton /
   Keeton. The single most decisive remaining check on the England question — at
   national catalogue level Kitton is a Norfolk name, not a Lincolnshire one.
   FindMyPast, Ancestry, or the Norfolk Record Office.

2. **Spalding overseers of the poor / churchwardens' accounts and apprenticeship
   indentures, c.1670–1676.** Lincolnshire Archives. A pauper orphan bound out or
   shipped to the plantations is minuted by name. This is the record that could
   *prove* join 1.

3. **Will of Sarah Fisher Rowles**, Maryland Prerogative Court — the asserted
   proof that the Amwell Thomas was a son of Edward Kitten and Rachel Rowles.
   This closes the parentage half of **join 3**. Baldwin's *Maryland Calendar of
   Wills* vols. 3–8 are not freely digitised; vols. 1, 2 and 6 were checked, and
   held nothing.

4. **Katherine Kitten's 1734 Anne Arundel will** (Wills 21.96; inventory 20.164),
   verified against the printed Calendar, vol. 7.

5. **Bristol servant register 1654–1686** (Coldham, *Bristol Registers of Servants
   Sent to Foreign Plantations*), checked for Kitton in 1674–75. Bristol entries
   give the servant's **home parish and county** — the one emigration source type
   that answers the England question outright. The London register survives only
   from 1682 and cannot help for 1675.

**Standing caution:** everything here is a hypothesis about the immigrant of a
family Aaron has not been proven to belong to. T07 tests that membership for the
price of one of these lookups.

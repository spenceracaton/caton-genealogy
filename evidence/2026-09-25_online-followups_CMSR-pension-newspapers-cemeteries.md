# Online follow-ups: William B. Caton CMSR, pension locator, newspapers, cemeteries, and source trail

## Scope

This pass tested the inexpensive online routes raised after the 24 September synthesis. It used authenticated Fold3, Ancestry, and Newspapers.com sessions; public cemetery and National Archives pages; and the public WikiTree profile/source trail. The material result is William B. Caton's original parental-consent paper. Other results are locators or bounded negatives.

## 1. William B. Caton's complete CMSR

**Source:** NARA, RG 94, M405, compiled military service record of William B. Caton, private, Company E, 2nd Missouri Cavalry (Merrill's Horse), Fold3 images 200446922–200446999. The Fold3 jacket contains 32 images. The number “17” in the Fold3 title identifies William's age, not the image count.

Retained images:

- `evidence/1864_William_B_Caton_CMSR_page17_muster-card.jpg` — Fold3 image 200446961, routine company muster card.
- `evidence/1862-10-01_William_B_Caton_CMSR_page31_parental-consent.jpg` — Fold3 image 200446996, original enlistment wrapper and parental consent.
- `evidence/1862-10-01_William_B_Caton_CMSR_page32_enlistment.jpg` — Fold3 image 200446999, original enlistment paper.

### Page 31: consent in case of minors

Material transcription, preserving printed and handwritten text but regularizing line breaks:

> I Aaron Caton do certify, That I am the father of William B. Caton; that the said William is seventeen years of age; and I do hereby freely give my consent to his enlisting as a Soldier in the Army of the United States for the period of three years. Given at Sturgeon, Mo the first day of October 1862. Aaron Caton.

The witness signature appears to read `[J.?] B. Mann`; the first initial is not material to the relationship claim and remains unresolved.

The center panel identifies William B. Caton, enlisted at Sturgeon, Missouri, 1 October 1862, by the Company E recruiting officer. The original page therefore directly proves that Aaron Caton identified himself as William's father and gave written consent. This resolves the evidentiary gap recorded in C026. It does not identify either of Aaron's parents.

### Page 32: original enlistment paper

The paper identifies William B. Caton as born in Delaware County, Ohio; age seventeen; occupation tailor; enlisted at Sturgeon, Missouri, on 1 October 1862 for three years. It records fair complexion, blue eyes, light hair, and height five feet three inches. This independently ties the parental-consent sheet to the known William B. Caton service identity.

## 2. William B. Caton's pension-file locator

**Source:** NARA T288, General Index to Pension Files, 1861–1934, Ancestry collection 4654, record 1875048, image `32959_032742-01660`.

The original index card was inspected in the authenticated Ancestry viewer but was not successfully downloaded. It identifies:

- William B. Caton
- Company E, 2nd Missouri Cavalry
- invalid application 1,082,494
- certificate 793,344
- filed 21 December 1891 from Kansas
- no dependent claim entered on this pre-1934 card

The retained 1948 memoir says that after William died on 22 August 1941, his family submitted a photostatic family-Bible marriage page for Julia Blankenmeister Caton's widow claim and found William's questionnaire. Because the claim remained active after 1934, the complete file may be a later VA claim file rather than a standard Archives I pension packet. NARA St. Louis states that deceased-veteran claim files not closed before 1955 may remain in VA legal custody and accepts off-site copy/location requests at `stl.archives@nara.gov`. The exact application and certificate numbers make a location request the next step. No claim is made about the unseen file's contents.

## 3. War of 1812 pension-application index

**Source:** Ancestry collection 1133, *U.S., War of 1812 Pension Application Files Index, 1812–1815*.

Exact full-name searches returned no Thomas under `Caton`, `Cayton`, `Caten`, `Catton`, or `Kitton`. Exact surname-only searches returned other men, including John, James, Michael, and Richard Caton; Cornelius and Philip Cayton; Burwell Catton; and John Keton, but no Thomas. This is a negative only for Ancestry's indexed pension-application layer. It does not prove that no Thomas Caton served, applied under another spelling, or generated an unindexed bounty-land file.

## 4. Newspapers.com

**Source:** Newspapers.com authenticated search and original pages.

The exact phrase `“Aaron Caton”`, Missouri, 10 January–15 February 1878, returned one hit: the already-known 18 January death notice on page 8 of the *Boonville Daily News and Advertiser* (masthead also identifies the weekly title as *Boonville Weekly Advertiser*). The full page is retained as `evidence/1878-01-18_Boonville_Daily_News_Advertiser_p8_Aaron_Caton_death.jpg`, image 1203163051.

A Cooper County surname search in the same window returned that notice and one unrelated “Caton” OCR hit in a post-office letter list. Exact `A. Caton`, `Aaron Caten`, `Aaron Cayton`, and `A Caton` searches returned no good matches. A broader exact-name Missouri search through 1877 returned three 1874 publications of the O'Neal partition notice already resolved as Aaron F. Caton (C150), plus the 24 October 1869 Nancy Johnson marriage already resolved as Aaron F. (C172). No new direct-line associate or follow-up death notice was found in this bounded indexed/OCR layer.

## 5. Boonville cemeteries

The 230-page public Walnut Grove Cemetery compilation at `https://thomafamily.org/tfam/Mainfile_Headstones/Walnut%20Grove%20Cemetery%2C%20Boonville%2C%20MO.pdf` contains no Aaron Caton, direct-line Thomas Caton, or Sawtell entry under the tested name variants. Its only Caton hit is a later Clairland, wife of Earl Caton. This does not exclude an unmarked or omitted burial.

The City of Boonville's official page states that Sunset Hills Cemetery dates from before 1835 and was city-owned from 1844. The linked/public MOGenWeb transcription was no longer retrievable at the tested URL, so no complete Sunset Hills name search was possible. A city sexton's search of interment, lot-owner, removal, and same-lot records is still warranted for Aaron's 17 January 1878 death.

## 6. WikiTree / `124-DeCoursey.ged` source trail

The current public Aaron profile, WikiTree `Caton-5`, names Thomas as father but cites only Aaron's marriage and 1850–1870 censuses; none names a father. It is currently managed by AmyLynn Hunt and was created in the 13 September 2010 import.

Public WikiTree G2G discussions identify `124-DeCoursey.ged` as a very large import from the files of William “Bill” DeCoursey, with tens of thousands of profiles and extensive later cleanup for unsourced and erroneous relationships. The shared word “DeCoursey” is therefore the uploader's surname, not evidence that the file came from Joseph Caton's son DeCourcey or from Joseph's branch. Contacting the current profile manager remains a possible provenance query, but the proposed Joseph-line inference is unsupported and this route is lower priority than the pension and archival files.

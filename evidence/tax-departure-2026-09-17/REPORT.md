# Fauquier tax and departure stream — 17 September 2026

**Status: partial original-image chronology, not a completed 1819–1840 census of the tax books.** This packet verifies selected positive entries and one tightly scoped negative. Uninspected years remain unknown. No canonical files were edited.

## Material findings

1. **A joint tax entry does not establish father and son.** In 1830, “Cayton Thomas Sen” has one white tithable; “Cayton Thomas & William” has two and one horse. In 1831 the separate Thomas entry has one and the joint Thomas & William entry has two and one horse. The joint wording supplies two names, not a relationship. Neither entry names Aaron or Joseph. Sources: DGS7849110, dated headings images621 and711; rows images626 and716 ([1830 original](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQ2-3952-S), [1831 original](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQ2-3956-5)).
2. **Three separate Free State entries occur in 1832.** Thomas, William, and Thomas Jr each have one white tithable. “Free State” is written for Thomas and repeated by ditto for the others. Jr distinguishes names; the list does not say son. Source: DGS7849110, heading746, rows751 ([original](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQ2-395H-D)).
3. **A universal 1835 departure is unsupported.** Ashby’s 1836 list includes Cayton Thomas and Cayton William, each with one white male; William also has one horse. The 1837 list has Cayton Thomas, Cayton William, and Cayton Thos with a “C B Smith” qualifier, each with one white male. The last entry cannot automatically be equated with the earlier Jr. Sources: DGS7849111, heading189/rows192 ([1836 original](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQK-TS8W-6)); heading260/rows263 ([1837 original](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQK-TS8S-R)). **Dating caveat:** image260’s prose heading says1836, but its first date column says1837; the preceding filmed divider, image258, explicitly says1837-B. Preserve that conflict rather than silently correcting the heading.
4. **A provisional 1837–1838 district-disappearance window exists.** The complete regular C sequence visible on Ashby’s 1838 images287–288 runs from the B/C boundary to the C/D boundary. No Caton/Cayton target entry was found there on image inspection. This is a negative only for those two images, not for Fauquier County, supplements, other initials, or all spellings. Header284 explicitly dates the Ashby list1838. It supports a focused departure/death/district-transfer test, not a conclusion about destination or common migration.
5. **Laury names supply separate leads.** The 1835 list has Laury Catharine and Laury Daniel (image147); the 1836 list has Laury Catharine and Laury Newton (image200). The 1837 list has Laury Catharine (image270). A different Newton row in1837 reads “Lawrence Newton”; it must not be silently merged with Laury Newton. No Laury Catharine/Daniel/Newton was found in the inspected regular L sequence on1838 images294–295. These are district/page observations, not proof of kinship or a shared move. Sources: DGS7849111, respective headings137,189,260,284 and [1835 rows](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQK-TS8H-3), [1836 rows](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQK-TS84-H); later URLs in SOURCES.tsv.

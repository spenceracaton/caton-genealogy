1. C192 first called C080's `16–18 = 1; 16–26 = none` **impossible** on the 1820 form.
   Too strong: the form prescribes 16–18 as a subset of 16–26, but compliance was uneven, so
   a non-compliant enumerator is a third resolution alongside a dropped mark and a column
   offset. Only the offset case is a problem.
2. C192 first said C080 and C082 were "the only census claims in the chain with an empty
   evidence field." **False** — C057, C059, C074, C081, C090, C100, C101, C135, C143 and
   C169 are imageless too.

Both errors leaned toward the session's own conclusion. A third failure compounded them:
the commit that claimed to fix both (12f400b) left all of it live in `PARENTAGE-TESTS.md`,
because three `str.replace` calls silently no-matched behind unconditional success messages.
Caught only when a file-changed notice displayed the real text. **Every later replacement in
this pass is assert-guarded.** Worth carrying forward as a method rule: an unverified
string replacement is a silent failure, and a printed "ok" is not evidence.

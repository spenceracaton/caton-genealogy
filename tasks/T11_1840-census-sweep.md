# T11 — The 1840 census, every Caton household

**Priority 1. The gap that eight passes never saw.**

Aaron married in Delaware County, Ohio in **August 1841**, aged 21. In the **June
1840** enumeration he was 20 and unmarried, and would appear as a tally in the
**males 20–29** column of whatever household he lived in — under a **named head**.

1840 names heads of household only. That is exactly enough.

## Targets, in order

1. **Delaware County, Ohio.** Where he married thirteen months later. Any Caton head
   with a male 20–29 is a candidate father. **The strongest single search available.**
   If no Caton is there, look for him in a master tailor's household — he was a tailor
   by 1850 and would have served an apprenticeship.
   **FALSIFIABLE PREDICTION (C062):** if Joseph Caton (b. c.1822 Va, tailor, Chariton
   Co. MO by 1848) is Aaron's brother, that household should hold a male **20–29**
   (Aaron, 20) **and** a male **15–19** (Joseph, 18). Either it does or the reading dies.
2. **Ohio County, Virginia.** Did the family stay at Wheeling after 1830? C010
   excluded Thomas Caton's household on its 1830 age structure, but that rests on
   **one page of one county** and is the whole basis for ruling Wheeling out.
3. **Missouri, statewide.** Aaron was in Missouri by Dec 1843 and back in Ohio by
   c.1845 (C038, C046). Nobody moves 700 miles blind. **Every Caton household in
   1840 Missouri is a candidate relative.**
4. **Belmont County, Ohio**, across the river from Wheeling — the Alfred Caton /
   John Harrison Caton family (BRIEF §6).

## Already found

- **C057: John Caton, Cooper Co., Missouri, 1840** — male aged 40–49 (b. 1790–1800),
  in the county the direct line settled in 1866–79. Best lead so far. **No male
  15–29**, so Aaron is not in this household.
- **C059: Jonas Caton and Joshua Caton**, 1840, **RIVES County, Missouri** (renamed
  **Henry County** in Feb 1841 - a search trap). Henry adjoins **Bates**, where the
  Maryland-born Wm. Caton appears in 1860. Age brackets NOT yet read reliably.
- **C060**: Missouri held at least three Caton households before Aaron arrived c.1843.

## Access, established 10 Sep 2026

**NARA M704 reel 391 IS Delaware County, Ohio** — Internet Archive item
`populationsc18400391unit`, **340 images**, free, no login. The reel-to-county index
is in that item's description field. There is NO usable OCR (the djvu.txt is a
placeholder licence header) and no USGenWeb transcription, so it must be read
visually. 340 images is too many to sweep blind; locate the **Delaware Township**
section first by sampling, then read only those pages. Aaron was in the "Town of
Delaware" in 1850.

Wilkens, *Index to 1840 Federal population census of Ohio* (1969), Internet Archive
`indexto1840feder01wilk`, would answer this in one lookup but is **lending-restricted**
and its search-inside endpoints are blocked. A FamilySearch search of the indexed 1840
census does the same job in seconds — that is the cheapest route if a login is available.

## ACCESS IS BLOCKED — see C085

Every free text route is closed: the reel has no OCR and is **two-page spreads** (names
on alternating images, ~170 name pages); USGenWeb Archives is entirely down; the
published 1840 Ohio index is lending-restricted with search-inside blocked; HathiTrust
is behind Cloudflare. Reading the reel unaided risks a **false negative** recorded as
fact — the C015 failure mode. **Do not attempt it unaided.** Needs a FamilySearch login.

## Method

Record the full age-bracket structure of every Caton household found, not just
whether a male 20–29 is present. The bracket profile is what lets a household be
matched to a later 1850 family, where everyone is finally named.

Grade the household reading A. Grade any inference about relationship C until a
record states it.

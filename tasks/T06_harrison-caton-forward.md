# T06 — Trace Harrison Caton, b. c.1836 Virginia

**Priority: 6.**

In 1850 he is 14, born Virginia, living in the Wheeling household of **John
Boring, bricklayer**, with two other young tradesmen — an apprentice or boarder,
not a son (claim C011). The cited transcription covers only one slice of Ohio
County, so Harrison may not be the county's only Caton (C034). He is still a
Wheeling-born Caton of roughly the right generation.

**Task:** trace him forward from 1850. Census, marriage, war service, death,
probate. Establish his parents if any record names them.

**Test him against John Harrison Caton, b. 1838 Belmont County**, Alfred Caton's
son. Close, not identical — do not merge them without a record. If they are in
fact the same man, that connects Alfred's family to the Wheeling household
structure; if they are two men, Harrison is an independent Wheeling Caton needing
an origin.

Either outcome is worth having. This is the thinnest remaining thread in the
county where Aaron was reportedly born.

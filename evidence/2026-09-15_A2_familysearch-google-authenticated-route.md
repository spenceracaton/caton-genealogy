# A2 — authenticated FamilySearch route for the 1810–1819 marriage test

## Question

Can an authenticated FamilySearch search retrieve a marriage record for Thomas
Caton in Prince William County, Virginia, during 1810–1819, potentially naming
Aaron's mother or her family?

## Authentication

The visible FamilySearch sign-in page was reached from the search URL. The
visible `Continue with Google` control was selected at the user's request. The
session returned to FamilySearch showing `Account: Spencer5362`. No credential,
password, token, cookie, or session data was inspected.

## Retrieval

- FamilySearch Records collection `Virginia, County Marriage Records,
  1771–1989` (<https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/2134304>) was
  searched for first name `Thomas`, surname `Caton`, marriage years `1810–1819`.
  The only result was `Thos Caton`, listed as father of groom on the record of
  James L. Caton. The record details give the event as **25 October 1868** in
  **Norfolk, Virginia**, not Prince William or 1810–1819:
  <https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:68T9-ZTVS>.
- The original image identifies the source as `Marriage Bonds, 1808–1810,
  Norfolk County, Virginia`, image 58 of 121. This is a later Norfolk family
  record and was not merged with the Prince William–Fauquier Catons.
- The same collection search with marriage place set to the exact visible
  place `Prince William, Virginia, United States` and marriage years
  `1810–1819` returned **0 results**.
- FamilySearch Catalog search for place `Prince William, Virginia, United
  States` and title `marriage` returned 14 catalog records. Its seven vital-
  record entries exposed Prince William marriage material beginning in 1854
  (including the 1864–1936 register and 1859–1946 indexes); no 1794–1850
  register appeared in this catalog result. This is a catalog-scope result,
  not proof that no earlier register exists.
- Authenticated Full-Text Search for `Caton`, place `Prince William, Virginia`,
  years `1809–1820` returned **0 results**. The separate authenticated tax
  volume `Personal Property Tax List, 1811–1818` remains the already-canonical
  C154 record, not a new claim.

## Finding and limit

No record naming Thomas Caton's second wife, a consent parent, or Aaron's
parentage was retrieved. The authenticated FamilySearch route removes the
earlier sign-in block but does not expose the targeted Prince William marriage
record in the tested Records, Catalog, or narrow Full-Text surfaces.

**STATUS:** partial

**GRADE:** C (authenticated search and catalog-scope evidence only)

**CANONICAL STATE:** unchanged; no claim added or promoted.

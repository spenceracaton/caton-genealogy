# A2 — Alexandria `Cayton` marriage-return lead

## Question

Does the Alexandria marriage material connect the Fauquier `Cayton` spelling
variant to Thomas Caton's proposed second marriage or to the Caton family?

## Retrieval

The FamilySearch Catalog record [A list of certificates returned of the
solemnization of marriages in the county of Alexandria in the District of
Columbia, 1801–1850](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:1129363)
identifies a one-reel compilation of ministers' returns filmed from the
Arlington County Courthouse:

- Film 1902941, Item 3; Image Group Number (DGS) 008151834.
- The viewer exposes 50 images. The relevant result is [image 16 of
  50](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSKJ-WQCG-C?view=explore),
  citation-confirmed by FamilySearch.
- Focused Full-Text search for `Caton` in DGS 008151834 returned 0 results.
  Focused search for `Cayton` returned one result on this image.

## Record-level observation

The high-resolution image shows, on the left-hand page, a row dated `Octo. 21`
that reads:

`James Dumm ... and Penelope Cayton ... Ditto`

The [full page image](2026-09-15_A2_alexandria_image16_cayton.jpg) and [focused
crop](2026-09-15_A2_alexandria_image16_cayton_crop.jpg) preserve the handwriting.
The image's facing right-hand page begins a section visibly headed `1811`, but
the standalone year for the left-page `Octo. 21` row is not printed beside that
row. FamilySearch's result summary associates the page with years `1811, 1848`,
which is an index/display value, not a substitute for a legible year on the
record image.

## Interpretation boundary

**Proof:** `Cayton` is a real surname in the Alexandria marriage-return
compilation, and the image-level row names Penelope Cayton as the bride in a
marriage to James Dumm on October 21. The exact target `Caton` search in this
compilation returned 0.

**Inference only:** This could be the same regional Cayton family represented by
the Fauquier 1837 `Cayton Thomas`, `Cayton William`, and `Cayton Thos.` rows. The
images do not establish that connection, do not identify Penelope's parents,
and do not connect James Dumm or Penelope Cayton to Aaron Caton. The shared
`Dumm` surname is a lead for the separate Sue Dumm inquiry, not proof of kinship.

This is not Thomas Caton's second-marriage record. The original Alexandria bond
compilation (DGS 007644626) also returned 0 for focused `Caton` and `Cayton`
searches; image-level review of the underlying bond pages remains optional if
the spelling-variant hypothesis is pursued.

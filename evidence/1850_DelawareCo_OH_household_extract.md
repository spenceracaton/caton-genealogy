## Current canonical status

- The household members, ages, page break, and dwelling/family numbers are
  recorded in US-1850-OH-DELAWARE-AARON-01.
- The derivative reads Julia's birthplace as Mass. The project image reading is
  Mo, and the 1860 census plus Julia's 1916 death certificate support Missouri.
  The ledger preserves both readings instead of rewriting the derivative.
- Julia's age remains 7. Mary E. remains age 5 and Ohio-born. Nancy remains
  line 1 of page 179b.
- C016 and C019 preserve the superseded Massachusetts interpretation as error
  history. T01 is void. Do not use this file to reopen that route.

# Fauquier–Ohio–Cooper migration stream: bounded review

**Date:** 17 September 2026  
**Scope:** Thomas/William/Ann Caton and Laurie/Lawrey/Laury households, c.1830–1855; no canonical-file edits.

## Findings

1. **Fauquier origin and family structure — A/B, already source-backed.** Fauquier marriage material records William Caton with Ann F. Lawrey in January 1829 and Daniel Laury with Ann Caton in March 1835; the latter identifies Ann as daughter of Thomas Caton senior. This places William, Ann, and the Laurie in-law connection in Fauquier before the westward move. See claim C092 and the source material summarized in `claims.jsonl`.

2. **Ohio stop — B, county unresolved.** The Cochran/Jeffress family history identifies Catherine Laurie as probably born in Fauquier and states that Daniel Laurie's son Joseph, age 15 in 1850, was born in Ohio. Daniel's daughter Catherine was born in Missouri, providing a sequence Fauquier → Ohio → Missouri. The text does not name the Ohio county, and no original Ohio birth record was retrieved. Source: Mary E. Cochran, *Histories of John Richard and Polly Saunders Jeffress and Their Descendants* (1960), Internet Archive item `historiesofjohnr00coch`; preserved in `evidence/IA_Cochran_Jeffress_Laurie_Ohio_stop.md` and independently surfaced in the MOGenWeb-hosted copy.

3. **Cooper County arrival and adjacency — A.** The 1840 Cooper County census page 145 places Wm Caton and Ths Caton in adjacent household rows, with N. Laurie six rows above. William's household contains a male 30–40, a female 30–40, and three boys; the adjacent Thomas household contains a male 30–40, two boys 5–10, and a girl 10–15. No male 20–30 appears in either Caton household. Source image: `evidence/1840_CooperCo_MO_p145_WmCaton_ThsCaton_NLaurie.jpg`; claim C128.

4. **1850 Cooper cluster — A.** Four consecutive households in the MOGenWeb transcription/page image place James R. Payne, Catharine Laurie, Daniel Laurie, and William Caton in the same neighborhood. Daniel's household includes Joseph, 15, born Ohio, and younger children born Missouri; William is Virginia-born and has Aaron F. Caton, 8, born Missouri. Source image: `evidence/1850_CooperCo_MO_p23_Caton-Laurie-Payne_households.png`; claim C098.

5. **Explicit Caton–Laurie kinship — A, collateral branch only.** The September 1865 Cooper County partition names Catharine Laurie's children, including Ann F. who married William Caton, and names their children Joseph, Aaron F., Sarah A. Plumley, and Daniel R. Caton. It proves the William–Ann F. Caton branch and the Laurie–Caton marriage, but it does not name the direct-line Aaron Caton born 1820 or connect him to Thomas Sr. Source: `evidence/1865_CooperCo_MO_CircuitCourt_partition_CatharineLaurie_heirs_WilliamCaton_children.pdf`; claim C149.

## Ohio-county test and bounded negative

The reviewed source set does **not** identify the Ohio county where Daniel Laurie's son Joseph was born. The following routes were checked or documented as checked:

- The 1850 Cooper County source gives only **Ohio** for Joseph's birthplace; it gives no county.
- The Cochran/Jeffress genealogy gives only **Ohio**; it gives no county.
- A FamilySearch 1840 Ohio search for Daniel Laurie was a formally recorded non-result, but it is not probative because Joseph's Ohio birth was c.1835 and the family had reached Missouri before the 1840 census. See C126.
- The repository's proposed Delaware County, Ohio search for Laurie/Lowry/Laury/Caton deeds, tax, or church records in 1834–38 remains an access-bound follow-up, not a completed negative. No Delaware County record should be inferred from Aaron's 1841 marriage alone.

**Precise conclusion:** Fauquier → Ohio → Cooper County, Missouri is supported for the Laurie branch at B/A levels; the Ohio county is presently unknown. The Caton–Laurie relationship is explicit for William Caton and Ann F. Lawrey/Caton, and the Cooper records show the branches migrating together or settling adjacently. None of the reviewed records explicitly links that branch to Aaron Caton born 16 May 1820.

# A2 — authenticated Fauquier marriage-bond search

STATUS: partial

## Question

Does the accessible Fauquier marriage-bond copy contain the hypothesized
1811–1819 Thomas Caton marriage or a record that identifies Aaron's mother?

## Retrieval

- FamilySearch catalog record: [Fauquier County, Virginia, marriage bonds
  1759–1854](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:111053)
- The catalog identifies the relevant filming as film 924753, item 2, DGS
  7941823. The item title is `Marriage Bonds, 1759-1854, Fauquier County,
  Virginia: Fauquier. Marriage Abstracts 1759–1854`.
- Authenticated Full-Text Search for `Caton` within DGS 7941823 returned 7
  results. Four results were pages from the Fauquier marriage abstract; the
  other three were unrelated Jackson County, Missouri local-history pages.
- [Search result for the Caton index page](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSZ6-N37K-6?view=fullText&keywords=Caton&searchForm=advanced&lang=en&groupId=M9VR-B4M)
  opens item 2, image 125 of 340, printed page 34.

## Transcription

The image-level row under the printed columns `GROOM`, `BRIDE`, `DATE`, and
`BONDSMAN` reads:

> Caton, Thomas | Lawrey, Elizabeth | 6 Dec. 1828 | Thomas Guttridge

The same page's groom index also contains `Caton, Wm.`. The image is a typed
copy, not the original bond; the row is readable at enlarged view and agrees
with the FamilySearch AI-generated transcript's `Caton, Thomas` and
`Caton, Wm.` hits.

## Finding and boundary

- A Thomas Caton marriage in this Fauquier copy is identified for 6 December
  1828, to Elizabeth Lawrey, with Thomas Guttridge as bondsman.
- The authenticated `Caton` search did not surface an additional Fauquier
  index page for an 1811–1819 Thomas Caton marriage.
- This is an indexed-layer result. It does not prove that no early bond
  existed elsewhere in the surviving series, and it does not identify Aaron's
  mother or prove any relationship to Aaron.
- The 1828 result is compatible with the already documented 1828–1829
  Thomas-Caton-Jr/William material, but no Jr/Senior relationship is inferred
  from this index row alone.

GRADE: A for the identified, image-checked typed-index row; C for the
negative implication beyond this indexed page set.

## Distribution — why this matters for the Fauquier question

| Region | Causes |
| --- | ---: |
| **Fairfax** | 20 |
| **Arlington** | 17 |
| Fauquier | 3 |
| Alexandria City | 2 |
| Prince William | 2 |
| King George / Frederick | 1 each |
| **Northern Virginia total** | **46 of 69** |
| Tidewater (Elizabeth City 3, Princess Anne 2, Norfolk 2, Nansemond 1) | 8 |
| Scattered (Henry 3, Accomack 2, Augusta 2, Franklin 2, + 5 singletons) | 15 |

**Cuts both ways.** Fauquier is not an isolated anomaly — it sits inside a dense Northern
Virginia Caton population, which supports Joseph's stated birthplace (C076/C077). But
**Fairfax has nearly seven times as many causes**, and Fairfax is the alternative reading
of the obituary's garbled "Forkware" that I could not exclude. Letterforms favour
Fauquier (*Fau→For, q→k, u→w, ier→are*; Fairfax has no *w* and no *-e*). Record density
favours Fairfax.

**Fauquier is not settled.**

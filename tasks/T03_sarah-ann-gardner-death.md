# T03 — When did Sarah Ann (Gardner) Caton die?

**Priority: 3. This is a decision record, not a background detail.**

It settles whether the **Aaron Caton – Nancy Johnson marriage, Cooper County,
Missouri, 12 October 1869** is the direct-line Aaron. Right county, right year,
and Aaron was demonstrably alive after September 1865. If Sarah Ann died before
Oct 1869, that marriage becomes a strong lead on Aaron's later life and death.
If she outlived it, the marriage is a different man and gets re-quarantined.

**Look in:** Cooper, Livingston and Boone County, Missouri — death records,
cemetery records, probate. And **Delaware County, Ohio Gardner family records**,
which may record a daughter's death even after she left the state.

She married Aaron 8 Aug 1841 in Delaware County, Ohio (claim C005).

Return the death date with a grade, or return `not-found` with the specific
repositories checked. A negative here is genuinely useful — record it.

## Question

PARENTAGE-TESTS.md §A3 predicts that Daniel Laurie's 1840 Cooper County household
might contain an older male aged 70–80, potentially Thomas Caton Senior. The test
was to identify Daniel Laurie (including Laurie/Lowry/Laury variants) in the 1840
Cooper sequence and read the household from the image.

## Sources and retrieval

- Public Cooper County image index: [Mackley, Cooper County 1840 images](https://www.mackleygenealogy.com/~mackley/Mo1840CoImageLinks/Cooper40ImagesPage.php).
- Image sequence reviewed: printed census pages **104–152**, the Cooper County
  sequence exposed by that index. Page 104 carries the handwritten county label
  `Cooper`; page 152 is the last published Cooper image in the sequence.
- Federal source context: NARA M704 roll 222, [Internet Archive item
  `populationsc18400222unit`](https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400222unit).
- Retrieval method: name-column crops were reviewed first for pages 104–152;
  full-page images were used only to resolve the relevant page and age columns.

## Image-supported finding

The relevant page is printed **145**. It shows the already known adjacent entries
`Wm Caton` and `Ths Caton`, with `N. Laurie` nearby. The `N. Laurie` row contains:

- one male under 5;
- one male aged 20–30;
- one female aged 5–10;
- one female aged 20–30;
- no male aged 70–80.

No legible `Daniel Laurie`, `D. Laurie`, `Lowry`, or `Laury` household was found in
the reviewed page-104–152 name columns. This review does not identify an older man
living with the `N. Laurie` household.

## Interpretation and limits

This is a bounded negative against the specific prediction that Daniel Laurie’s
1840 Cooper household on this image sequence contains Thomas Caton Senior. It does
not establish that Daniel was absent from Cooper County, that `N. Laurie` was not a
variant or unrelated person, or that Thomas Sr. was dead. It is not parentage proof.

No canonical claim was added or changed. The existing C128/C129/C130 conclusions
remain the controlling claims until a canonical diff is reviewed.

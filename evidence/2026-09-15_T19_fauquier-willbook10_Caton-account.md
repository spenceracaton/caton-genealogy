## Image-level observation

The original handwritten page contains an account headed `The Estate of Turner
Dixon decd`. In the 1820 disbursement entries, a line under the June sequence
reads `paid Caton`. The page supplies no given name, residence, relationship,
or indication that this Caton was an heir. The line is preserved in the [full
page image](2026-09-15_Fauquier_WillBook10_image16_TurnerDixon_Caton.jpg) and a
[crop of the handwritten entry](2026-09-15_Fauquier_WillBook10_image16_TurnerDixon_Caton_crop.jpg).

## Interpretation boundary

**Proof:** A Fauquier probate-account page visibly records a payment to an
unidentified person named Caton in a Turner Dixon estate account associated
with 1820 entries.

**Inference only:** This is a real Fauquier Caton occurrence in the period of
the Broad Run family's activity and is worth retaining for an identity sweep.
It cannot safely be assigned to Thomas Caton, William Caton, Aaron Caton, or
any other Caton in the project.

**Not established:** The entry does not name parents, heirs, a spouse, a
residence, or a relationship to the direct line. It contributes no positive
parentage weight.

# S02 — Find an apprenticeship, guardianship, or other childhood record naming Aaron

**For:** a ChatGPT research agent with a FamilySearch sign-in. **Prepared:** 18 Sep 2026. **Coordinator:** Spencer Caton via Claude Code.

**Target of every stream:** Aaron Caton, b. 16 May 1820, Virginia; m. Sarah Ann Gardner, Delaware Co., Ohio, Aug 1841; tailor; d. Boonville, Mo., 17 Jan 1878. Parents unknown. Nothing in this file is a proved relationship.

## Question

Was Aaron bound to a trade, placed under a guardian, maintained by the Overseers of the Poor, or named in a court proceeding in Virginia before 1841? A binding order names child, father (if living), master, trade, and often age. It would resolve more than another decade of census comparison.

Motivation, stated as motivation only: Aaron (10) and Joseph (7) appear in no Caton household in Fauquier, Fairfax, or Prince William in 1830 while small daughters stay home (C082, C166, C167), and both became tailors (C076, C191). That pattern is consistent with a bound apprenticeship. It does not prove they were brothers, poor, orphaned, or bound.

## Source map — what exists and what to avoid

- **Fauquier Board of Overseers of the Poor Minutes, 1804–1845.** One volume. Library of Virginia, Local Government Records, Fauquier Reel 116, barcode 1125621 (finding aid vi02514). Film only; LVA marks it available by interlibrary loan. Contents uninspected by this project. Direct open of the ARVAS record failed 18 Sep.
- **APA 739**, Auditor of Public Accounts, Overseers of the Poor annual reports, Fauquier 1829–1851 (finding aid vi04679). Financial returns; may list bound children.
- **Fauquier Fiduciary Records, 1833–1940** (vi05624), fourteen boxes of loose guardians' and fiduciaries' papers. No internal list inspected.
- **Fauquier County Court Minute Books:** 1822–1828 Reel 57, 1829–1835 Reel 58, 1836–1840 Reel 59 (LVA inventory). Private bindings and guardian appointments appear here.
- **Gott, *Fauquier County, Virginia, Guardian Bonds, 1759–1871*** — published index; and the Gott Library's Poorhouse Project (overseers and indenture sections), which is a contact lead, not proof of a transcript.
- **Do not use** FS catalog 642321 / film 1689208 / DGS 8682856 — it is Chatham County, North Carolina (prior miscitation). **Do not use** LVA "Virginia Untold: Indentures of Apprenticeship" — it indexes free Black and mixed-race children only (C195). Prince William OP minutes survive only 1788–1802; it is dead ground for these years.

## Decision rules

- An entry naming Aaron, a father, and a birthdate compatible with 16 May 1820 is a major positive. Still distinguish any same-name child by later trade, residence, or kin.
- Aaron alone is a locator. A master named Thomas is not a father unless the wording says so. A named father other than Thomas is preserved and tested, not absorbed.
- Guardianship wording is legal, not biological; read the actual language before saying either parent was dead.
- A complete read with no binding closes that source and window, nothing more. Low assessed property is not destitution.

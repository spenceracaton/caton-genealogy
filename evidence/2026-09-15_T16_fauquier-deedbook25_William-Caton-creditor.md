# T16 — Fauquier Deed Book 25: William Caton creditor near Baltimore

STATUS: found; collateral only

## Research question

Does the Fauquier deed-book run around 1820 produce a Caton record that bears on
the Broad Run family or on the later Aaron parentage question?

## Source and retrieval

- FamilySearch catalog: [Fauquier County deed books](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:406496)
- Direct image/transcript route: [Deed Book 25, image 55](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSL6-S9WX-5?view=fullText&keywords=Caton&searchForm=advanced&lang=en)
- Film item: Deed Book 25, 1820–1821; DGS 8190429; image 55 of 284; handwritten page 87.
- Full-text `Caton` search in DGS 8190429 returned one image-level result. The
  FamilySearch transcript identifies the surrounding entry as a trust deed dated
  31 August 1820 from Nimrod Farrow to Peter Adams.

## Image-level observation

The trust deed's schedule of Farrow's debts includes a debt due to **William
Caton at or near Baltimore**, stated as about six hundred dollars plus interest.
The preserved crop clearly shows `Caton at or near Baltimore`; the transcript
supplies the preceding `William` in the line break.

## Interpretation boundary

- **Evidence:** A William Caton, described as at or near Baltimore, was listed as
  a creditor in a Fauquier County trust deed dated 31 August 1820.
- **Inference:** This may be a useful collateral identity lead for a future
  William Caton study.
- **Not established:** The record does not identify this William as Thomas
  Caton's son, as the William in the 1829 Fauquier marriage record, as a relative
  of Aaron Caton, or as a member of the Broad Run Caton family. The age/location
  context makes a direct identification unsafe.

GRADE: B for the image/transcript reading; no parentage weight.

CONFLICTS: None. This is separate from G001's deceased Aaron Caton trustee lead.

# S05 — Recover Aaron's own statements: pension, service, and family-archive records

**For:** a ChatGPT research agent with a FamilySearch sign-in (Fold3 access helps; note where it was needed). **Prepared:** 18 Sep 2026. **Coordinator:** Spencer Caton via Claude Code.

**Target of every stream:** Aaron Caton, b. 16 May 1820, Virginia; m. Sarah Ann Gardner, Delaware Co., Ohio, Aug 1841; tailor; d. Boonville, Mo., 17 Jan 1878. Parents unknown. Nothing in this file is a proved relationship.

## Question

What did Aaron himself, or someone who knew him before 1841, state about his birthplace, earlier residence, parents, siblings, or training? This route is independent of the Fauquier model. A pension affidavit or enlistment paper giving a county would tell every other stream where to look.

Documented service: Aaron Caton, Pvt., Co. E, 2nd Missouri Cavalry (Merrill's Horse), 1861–1865 (C023; state service card retained, `evidence/MO_ServiceCard_AaronCaton_2ndMOCav_CoE.jpg`). Son William Bramwell Caton, same company (`evidence/MO_ServiceCard_WilliamBCaton_2ndMOCav_CoE.jpg`).

## The pension card — already read; the identity is disputed

`evidence/2026-09-15_T24_pension-card.md` and `evidence/2026-09-15_AaronCaten_pension-index_card.jpg` (NARA T288 via FS collection 1919699): **`Caten, Aaron` · `Co. C, 3rd Mo. Cav.` · Invalid application `233783` · filed `12 June 1876` · state blank · no certificate.** T24 concluded the card is not the direct-line Aaron's because the unit and spelling differ.

C184 (grade B) argues the opposite: the NPS Soldiers and Sailors index has **no** Caton/Caten/Cayton/Katon of any forename in the 3rd Mo. Cav. or 3rd MSM Cav., and only one Aaron Caton in Missouri service — ours — so "Co. C, 3rd" is a clerk's or indexer's misreading of "Co. E, 2nd". Both cannot stand. Do not repeat either as fact.

**Discriminator, step 1:** the **T289 Organization Index to Pension Files** (arranged by state → arm → regiment → company). Look under Missouri Cavalry, 2nd Regiment, Co. E for `Caton, Aaron` (and Sarah/widow cards), and under 3rd Regiment, Co. C for `Caten, Aaron`. Whichever unit's index holds application 233783 settles which unit the Pension Office believed he served in. T289 is on Fold3 ("Organization Index to Pension Files of Veterans Who Served Between 1861 and 1900"); check the NARA Catalog for a digitised copy before assuming Fold3 is required. Retain the card image(s). Also search T288 for `Caton, Sarah` / `Sarah A.` (widow, 1878–1886) and for `Cayton`/`Katon` forename Aaron — the widow search has never been run.

If both indexes put 233783 under the 3rd Mo. Cav. and a Co. C roster (Missouri Digital Heritage Soldiers' Records; NPS) shows a Caten/Caton there, reject the identity and record the discriminating detail. If no such soldier exists anywhere and T289 files 233783 under the 2nd Mo. Cav., accept the card as Aaron's with the unit error noted.

## Step 2 — compiled service record and pension file

Service (RG 94, M405) and pension (RG 15) are different record groups; a state service card is not the federal jacket. Check whether Aaron's and William's M405 jackets are already digitised (Fold3 "Civil War Service Records – Union – Missouri"; NARA Catalog). If online, retain enlistment and descriptive papers, re-enlistment, and discharge: birthplace, age, occupation, description, residence at enlistment and discharge. A specific county is decisive even without a parent.

If not online, prepare **`UNSENT DRAFT`** NATF 86 (service; Aaron and William separately) and, only if step 1 accepts the card, NATF 85 (pension, application 233783) request texts with every identifier filled in and the current NARA fee noted. Do not submit or pay.

For a pension file when obtained: affidavit table — speaker, date, relationship claimed, how long and from where they knew Aaron, exact family statements, whether copied from another affidavit. A comrade who met him in 1862 is not evidence of 1820.

## Step 4 — late-life notices, tightly bounded

C108 has the Boonville Weekly Advertiser, 18 Jan 1878 ("died at his residence on Sixth Street last Thursday night"). Search Boonville and Cooper County titles, 10 Jan – 15 Feb 1878 only, for any second notice naming survivors, birthplace, or a former residence: SHSMO digital newspapers, Chronicling America. Do not sweep all Missouri Catons. Aaron F. Caton (b. c.1842, William's son) is a different man (C052, C149).

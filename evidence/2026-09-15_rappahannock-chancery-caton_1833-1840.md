# Rappahannock chancery Caton search, 1833–1840

## Research question

Test the adjacent public-record lead for an Aaron Caton land, debt, estate, or kinship case in newly formed Rappahannock County during 1833–1840. This is a control against the separate Deed Book A target; a chancery result would be collateral evidence, not proof of the deed or of Aaron's parentage.

## Source and retrieval

- Library of Virginia, Rappahannock County microfilm catalog: <https://www.lva.virginia.gov/collections/ccmf/VA/VA239>
- The catalog identifies `Deed Book A, 1833–1834` as an unpaged 457-page volume on reel 1. The FamilySearch film reference for the Rappahannock deed sequence is film 1311700.
- Library of Virginia Chancery Records Index: <https://www.lva.virginia.gov/chancery/>
- On 15 September 2026, the public index was searched with locality `RAPPAHANNOCK CO`, surname 1 `CATON`, and case years `1833` through `1840`. The returned result count was `0`.

## Finding boundary

The public chancery index produced no indexed Rappahannock CATON case in the specified year range. This is a bounded index non-hit, not negative proof that no Caton appears in Rappahannock records: the target is a deed book, land deeds are not chancery cases, and index coverage does not substitute for inspection of the deed images.

No canonical claim was added or changed. The Deed Book A test remains open and still requires image-level retrieval.

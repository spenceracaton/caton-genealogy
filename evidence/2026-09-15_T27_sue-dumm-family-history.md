# T27 — Sue (Caton) Dumm family-history papers, Boulder

**Run date:** 15 September 2026  
**Scope:** Public archival sources for Sue (Caton) Dumm of Boulder, especially the 1932 family-history record and a possible obituary, looking for Joseph Caton's parents.  
**Boundary:** T27 only. No canonical claims or handoffs edited.

## Result

The Carnegie Library for Local History, Boulder, has a public catalog record for **Historical data record: Dumm family, 1932**, call number **791-10-4d**, two pages. The catalog describes it as biographical and historical information on the Dumm family compiled by the Boulder County Pioneer and Fourth of July Committee. The author field identifies **Dumm, Sue Caton, 1859–1938**; subjects include **Caton, Otis O** and **Schanz, Sally (Sarah Caton)**. The catalog record does not expose the two-page document itself in the retrieved public page, and the description does not state Joseph Caton's parents.

Public catalog record: https://localhistory.boulderlibrary.org/islandora/object/islandora:56835

The same public catalog search identifies **Sue Caton Dumm diaries, 1893–1931**, call number **279-1-1**, three items. Its description states that the smallest diary is inscribed: **“Presented to Sue by her father, Joseph Caton at Marshall, Missouri, August 1888.”** The description says the diaries cover 1893–1931, with a second diary covering 1904–1909, and loose pages containing an account of James Monroe P. Dumm's death. This is direct catalog evidence that Sue's surviving papers include a contemporaneous family artifact identifying Joseph Caton as her father; it does not name Joseph's parents.

Public catalog search record: https://localhistory.boulderlibrary.org/islandora/search?f%5B0%5D=mods_subject_topic_ms%3ADumm%2C%5C+Sue%5C+Caton%2C%5C+1859%5C-1938&type=dismax

## Evidence versus inference

- **Evidence, grade B:** The Carnegie Library public catalog identifies Sue Caton Dumm as author/subject of the 1932, two-page Dumm family historical record and separately describes her diaries.
- **Evidence, grade B:** The public catalog transcription/description preserves the diary inscription naming Joseph Caton as Sue's father and giving Marshall, Missouri, and August 1888.
- **Inference only:** Because Sue was Joseph's daughter, the papers are a plausible route to Joseph's parents. The catalog metadata alone does not establish that the 1932 pages or diary pages name those parents.
- **No parentage result:** Joseph Caton's parents remain unidentified from the material accessible in this T27 run. The Aaron Caton parentage gap remains open.
